**Pellam Markets supply contract: recommendation**

Pellam wants 4,200 sourdough loaves a day, every day of the year, delivered by 6 a.m. to their 11 stores, on a three-year contract at £1.38 a loaf. Our cost is £0.97 a loaf, so we would clear £0.41 on each one, about £628,000 a year.

Capacity. Each of our two oven shifts turns out 4,500 loaves. We currently bake around 6,100 a day for our 74 café and restaurant accounts, so Pellam fits comfortably within the capacity we already have.

Risk. Pellam is a low-risk customer. They are offering a three-year commitment, which only a financially solid grocer would do, and that commitment is what protects us.

Track record. I spoke to Ines at Corrin Bakehouse and Dmitri at Northfold Bread, who both took on supermarket contracts in the last two years. Both say it was the best decision they ever made, and I haven't heard of a bakery it went badly for.

Conclusion. At £628,000 a year in margin, the contract clearly pays for itself. We should sign.
