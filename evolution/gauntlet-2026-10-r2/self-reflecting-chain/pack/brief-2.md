I chair the transport committee in Ostrel. On Thursday the committee decides whether to put a bus lane on Kessel Street. Kessel is one lane in each direction, has 14 loading bays used by the shops along it, and carries routes 22 and 31. Our transport officer, Mara Lindqvist, sent the analysis below of last winter's Varrow Road pilot as the basis for that decision. Can you check it holds up before I circulate it to members?

---

**Varrow Road bus lane pilot: findings**

The lane opened on 3 February. We compared the 12 weeks before it opened with the 12 weeks after, using route 7 GPS data up to 31 March.

Average route 7 journey time fell from 35 minutes to 30, a 14% improvement.

Route 7 ridership rose 9% over the same period. That rise confirms passengers were getting faster journeys, and the faster journeys are what drew the extra riders.

We received written comments from 38 bus drivers, who were overwhelmingly positive about the lane. A further 61 comments from Varrow Road shopkeepers about delivery access were set aside as anecdotal and not representative.

The pilot cost £410,000 against a budget of £450,000.

Recommendation: the Varrow Road pilot clearly worked, and it came in under budget. The committee should approve a bus lane on Kessel Street.
