In the power industry, demand response means paying or signaling customers to reduce or shift their electricity use when the grid is stressed, such as during a heat-wave peak or a sudden supply shortfall, instead of building or firing up more generation. Participants, often large industrial users or aggregated households, get bill credits or payments for cutting load on request, or they sign up for time-varying prices that nudge them to use less at peak times. In irrigation terms, it's like offering growers an incentive to delay or curtail their water orders during peak-demand periods so you can manage capacity without expanding the system.
