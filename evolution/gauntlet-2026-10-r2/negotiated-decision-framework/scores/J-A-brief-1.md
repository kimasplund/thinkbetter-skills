# J-A scores, brief-1

- P4P: Covers the three gating items and the timing clash well. It also asks the sharp question of why the vote has to be this February for a fall 2028 move, and gives a dated plan. It never puts 640 against the ceiling explicitly, and it has no revenue-per-point arithmetic.
- V1B: Clear conditional recommendation. Notes the ~650 ceiling threshold and gives the revenue cost per point of re-enrolment, with its assumption flagged. Suggests a fall 2027 guarantee. "What would change it" names the cheapest check. Very actionable.
- R1E: Thorough. Notes that one-time proceeds don't fix a structural deficit, and gives a good order of steps for this week. Long, and the recommendation is spread across sections.
- G6T: Tight and decisive. Gets right that abstentions count against a 10-of-15 supermajority, checks 410+230=640 against the cap, and says nothing changes for 2027-28. The stakeholder table reads a little like a method artefact, but it is useful.
- L2B: Well ordered and adds the Vellacott rezoning terms. Notices that the January contracts cover a year when north is still open, and proposes a two-vote structure. Calling the 8% attrition a "margin" muddles the number.
- X3X: Strong content: legal vs physical capacity, conflicts and recusal, years of reserves, a two-stage vote. Too long and list-heavy for a busy head.
- V8N: Good idea to put the binding vote after the March 1 re-enrolment data. It overstates that "a few" lost students wipe out the savings, and calls Ardelle the "biggest donor signal" without support.
- A8S: Concise and decisive, with a good cheapest-first check. It also remembers the north-site neighbours. "Foundations usually accept this" is an overconfident generalisation.
- A1B: Flags the structural deficit, cash timing, and the bylaws and conflicts check. Very long. "Doubling" drop-off is wrong (the real increase is +56%).
- W3F: Good point that K-5 class-teacher staffing doesn't shrink with consolidation, and that the sale fixes cash but not the operating deficit. Misstates "640 students now plus the lower school". Somewhat long.
- J9A: Solid on the three gates, but advises aiming for abstention over opposition. With a 10-of-15 threshold an abstention counts the same as a no, so following that advice would cost votes.
- V9C: Careful tuition-loss example, with its assumption flagged, plus a check for other restricted gifts. Long, and the recommendation is less landed.
