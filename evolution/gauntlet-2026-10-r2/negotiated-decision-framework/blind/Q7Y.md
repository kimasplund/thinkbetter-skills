## Recommendation

Take a conditional "yes" to the Ironvale and Sorrelwood plan to the November 20 committee. Don't ask for approval to let Harrowfield go yet. The numbers favour the split (about 30% more rent against a 40% cut), but your premise that you need nothing from Harrowfield is wrong. Three things you do need could each undo the gain, and you can check all three before November 20.

Ask the committee for authority to sign the two leases once these conditions are met:

1. **The inline leases.** Read the co-tenancy clauses in all 26 shop leases. They were written in 2015 to "the usual grocery protections", and those usually let a shop cut its rent, pay a percentage of sales instead, or terminate if the anchor goes dark or is replaced by a non-grocery use. A gym and a clinic are probably not a grocer under that wording. If even a third of the shops can cut rent or leave, the extra rent from the two new tenants can disappear. Get a count of which shops, how much rent, and what the trigger is, before you present anything.
2. **The Harrowfield pad.** Harrowfield owns the fuel station outright and holds recorded rights over your entrances and parking. Pull the recorded easement and have counsel read it for:
   - any limit on changes of use, on parking ratios or on blocking access;
   - whether a 24-hour gym and a clinic with ambulance and imaging traffic need consent from the pad owner;
   - who controls the shared entrances.
   Harrowfield also has a strong reason to object. Its loyalty discount (10 cents a litre per $100 spent in-store) only works while it has a store on site. If you let the lease lapse, it loses that link and some of its fuel traffic, and it has no reason to be helpful. A tenant that is leaving and owns the front of your lot can slow you down, litigate or refuse a modification. Assume it will not cooperate for free.
3. **Marchbank's consent.** Both leases are over 15,000 sq ft, so both need the lender's sign-off. Routine consent was granted for grocery leases. A medical-imaging tenant and a 24-hour gym are different credit and use profiles, and the lender may care about the loss of a grocery anchor. Send Marchbank the term sheets now and get a written indication before you commit to anyone.

Two smaller checks. The parking claim ("rarely half full") is based on a grocery pattern. Count peak use at the times a gym (early morning and evening) and a clinic (weekday daytime) will stack, and include any exclusives or use clauses that restrict medical or fitness tenants. Also confirm whether Harrowfield holds a renewal option or any right to stay dark and keep paying, because the lease may end less cleanly than "it simply runs out".

## Who can block, and what each needs

|---|---|---|---|
| Investment committee | A higher, safe income stream and a plan that doesn't expose the trust to a wave of inline rent cuts | Declines, or approves then asks for a rework once the inline exposure surfaces | Gets the rent uplift, with the inline and consent risks quantified. Gives up a known anchor for a re-tenanted box. |
| Harrowfield | To keep its fuel business healthy, or to be paid for losing it. Its own sales are falling, so it also wants a graceful exit. | Uses its easement rights over entrances and parking, slow-walks cooperation, or lobbies your inline tenants | Gets a negotiated exit, a fuel-access guarantee and perhaps a short extension. | The 26 inline tenants | Customer traffic, and the rent protection they were promised | Co-tenancy rent cuts, termination, or quietly pushing back on renewals | Get a sales-driving replacement plan and, where needed, a rent concession. Give up the grocery draw. |
| Ironvale and Sorrelwood | The full space, workable parking and access, and an opening date | Walk away (the LOIs are not binding), or demand changes to terms | Get their space. Give up flexibility on timing, because lender consent and the easement checks come first. |

## How to bring each along

**Harrowfield.** Open this talk early. Offer a managed exit instead of a lapse: a short extension at agreed rent (or a lease-end date that matches the build-out), a written commitment that the fuel pad keeps full entrance and parking access, and a clean release of any claims. This costs you far less than a 40% rent cut across the anchor box, and it removes the one party that can disrupt the rest. Do it before you sign anything with the new tenants, and have counsel confirm what the easement does and doesn't let you do.

**Inline tenants.** Don't surprise them. Once you know the exposure, meet them as a group, or at least the largest and those with co-tenancy rights. Tell them what is replacing the anchor and the traffic you expect (gym members visit several times a week, and clinic visitors have time to spend). Where a clause would trigger, offer something in exchange for a waiver: a temporary percentage-rent period, marketing support, or a short rent holiday during the transition. Compare the cost of those concessions with the rent uplift, and only go ahead if the net still favours the plan.

**Ironvale and Sorrelwood.** Keep both warm and be honest about timing. Tell them the LOIs move to leases once consent and the easement checks clear, and offer exclusivity or a short option in return for a date. Ask each to share its peak-hour parking needs so the lot plan works for both. Have Sorrelwood confirm it needs its own access and signage, and check that neither use triggers restrictions from the other.

**Marchbank.** Send a short package: the two LOIs, tenant financials, the rent comparison and the plan for the inline tenants. Ask for written consent in principle.

**Committee.** Present the recommendation as a conditional approval, with dated checkpoints for the three conditions above and a fallback: if the inline exposure or the easement is worse than expected, go back to Harrowfield with a smaller concession than 40% as a competitive option, or run a hybrid with a smaller grocer or other anchor plus the clinic.

## Implementation notes

- Before November 20: read the inline leases and the easement, send Marchbank the term sheets, count peak parking and open the conversation with Harrowfield.
- If the answers come back clean, the committee can approve the two leases subject to Marchbank's consent and the Harrowfield agreement.
- Sign the leases only after the Harrowfield exit terms and lender consent are in writing, with closing dates that line up with the December expiry.

## What would change this recommendation

If a large share of inline rent is protected by co-tenancy clauses that a gym and a clinic won't satisfy, the plan stops paying for itself and a negotiated renewal with Harrowfield (even at a discount) may be the better choice. The cheapest first check is a one-page table from the 26 leases: which shops have grocery-specific protections, what happens when they trigger, and the annual rent at risk. Compare that number with the uplift from the two new tenants.
