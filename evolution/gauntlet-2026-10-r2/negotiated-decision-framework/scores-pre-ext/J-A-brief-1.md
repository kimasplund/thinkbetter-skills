# J-A scores, brief-1

V9C: Catches all four blockers (gift, Ardelle, ceiling, calendar). It also has a flagged per-student loss calculation and a two-step vote. Long, with a ten-item plan that partly repeats earlier sections. It suggests families are signing without knowing if "their child's campus will exist", which misreads things: the contracts are for a year when the north campus is still open.

A1B: Thorough and sound. Good non-obvious points: a trustee conflict-of-interest check with Vellacott, whether the deficit is structural, cash timing and bridge financing, and slack in the fall 2028 date. Usable dated timeline. Heavy on structure, and it spreads its recommendation across the whole reply instead of stating it once.

G6T: Clear conditional-resolution recommendation up front. Notes that nothing changes for 2027-28 and that six non-yes votes defeat the plan. It says what to do if the count comes in at eight or nine votes, and names the flip fact and the cheapest check. The stakeholder table is close to a framework look, but it reads as content. Compact.

L2B: Best for acting on. It gets the subtle point that contracts cover 2027-28 while north is still open, so the risk is perception. It adds Vellacott's LOI terms, a second bidder as leverage, a two-vote sequence, and separate fixes for the commute and for little kids sharing a campus with teenagers. Clear statement of what would change the recommendation. The "8% margin" wording is muddled.

P4P: Solid coverage and a useful dated timeline. Says "two and a half years" between the vote and fall 2028, which is wrong (about 18 months). It also suggests delaying the vote without weighing the build time an $8M building needs before fall 2028. Somewhat long.

V8N: Good idea to split the vote so the binding sale vote comes after the March 1 re-enrolment data, with tripwires. Flags its per-student revenue assumption. "Losing just a few students wipes out a large share of the savings" overreaches, since the savings were never quantified.
