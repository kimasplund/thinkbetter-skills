Notes (J-B, brief-1)

V8N: Clean, well-ordered treatment of the gift, the pledge, the ceiling and the calendar, with tripwires and a clear closing read. The ~$30k/student figure is derived and flagged, but "losing just a few of the 230 students wipes out a large share of the savings" overstates. It misses that the January contracts cover 2027-28, when north is still open.
P4P: Broad coverage and a useful dated timeline. It errs on the brief's timing: "two and a half years is long" when the vote-to-closure gap is about 19 months and an $8M build plus approvals makes it tight, not slack. "Probably going to collide" on the ceiling is a guess. It has no single recommendation.
L2B: The strongest insight. It ranks four sinkers, notes the contracts cover fall 2027 while north is still open, raises LOI rezoning conditions and a second bidder, and tests the $8M figure. It gives a clear recommendation, says what would flip it and names the cheapest check. Minor slips: the "8% margin" phrasing and a few unhedged generalisations ("typically", "certainly").
G6T: Tight and decisive: a conditional resolution, the 410-to-640 check against the ceiling, the "six non-yes votes defeats you" arithmetic, and a plan for an 8-or-9 vote. The stakeholder blocking table is visible mapping scaffolding. "Your financial case is strong" is asserted, not shown.
A1B: Thorough. It covers cash timing, bylaws and conflicts, temporary space and a structural deficit, and states its assumptions up front. It is heavily over-scaffolded (nine workstreams plus a timeline), and "doubling the number of students at drop-off" is wrong (410 to 640).
V9C: Good non-obvious points: other restricted gifts tied to north, proceeds net of fees and taxes, and rezoning as a second neighbour fight. Its numbers are flagged as illustrations. It says families sign "without knowing whether their child's campus will exist", but north stays open for 2027-28. It is long, with ten workstreams.
