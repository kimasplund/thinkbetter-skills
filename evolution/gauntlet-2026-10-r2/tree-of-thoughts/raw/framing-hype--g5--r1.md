Best option: keep the date and the marathon, and build the plan around an earlier start plus a heat-cancellation rule and more cooling (your option 5, strengthened). Four of the other five proposals break a constraint you described, and the sixth gives up too much.

**Why the others fail on your own numbers**

- **Mid-April.** Mornings would be cool, but the federation's qualifying window is 1 May to 30 September, so a race in April would not count for qualifying. Club runners chasing a time drive most of your 40% marathon growth, so you would probably lose them.
- **6:00 start.** The first Sunday train reaches Aldmere Central at 06:50, and about three-quarters of your runners come by train. Even with the 10-minute walk, almost nobody could be on the line at 6:00. It only works if the rail operator agrees to much earlier services, which is a big unknown.
- **Saturday 19:00.** The city's road closures are Sunday mornings only, 05:00 to 14:30. A six-hour cut-off from 19:00 runs to 01:00, and the trains and closures don't fit. It also clashes with the Saturday 5k on the towpath. The sponsor's enthusiasm doesn't change the permit.
- **Towpath for the middle 20 km.** The shade is attractive, but the path is 2.5 m wide. The navigation trust caps the 5k at 500 runners for that reason, and you would put up to 8,400 marathoners through it. That is a crowding and safety problem, and the trust is very unlikely to approve it. Dr Brandt's instinct about shade is right, but it needs a different form (see below).
- **Half only.** This cuts the heat exposure. But it removes the event that is growing and the qualifying draw, and the half runners are still out in the warm morning. I would treat it as a last resort.

**What I'd put to the board**

1. **Earlier start, but one the trains can serve.** Aim for about 7:15 to 7:30, not 6:00. A 7:30 start with the 06:50 train gives runners time to arrive and warm up, and a six-hour cut-off ends at 13:30, inside the 14:30 closure. Ask the rail operator now whether one or two earlier arrivals on race morning are possible. That could let you move to about 7:00. Consider starting the marathon first and the half later. Slower marathoners are the ones exposed to the 11:00 peak, so they should go earliest.
2. **A heat-cancellation or modification rule, set in advance.** Use a published trigger such as a forecast or measured WBGT threshold, with set responses: delay, shorten, convert to a fun run, or cancel. Insurers value pre-agreed triggers and decision-makers.
3. **More cooling, put where it matters.** Double the stations, but concentrate them in the final third of the marathon, where slower runners meet the 10:00 to 12:00 heat. Add shade, ice, sponges and misting, and medical cover that scales with the forecast.
4. **Use shade where it is feasible.** Ask the navigation trust whether a short towpath stretch, or a one-way, wave-limited use, is possible. Don't plan on it. Otherwise look for tree-lined or north-facing road sections.
5. **Runner communication.** Send heat advice, a pace-down message and a clear deferral option if the rule is triggered.

**Things to check before 18 November**

- Rail operator: can earlier trains run on race morning?
- Insurer: will they accept 7:15 to 7:30 plus the trigger rule and enhanced cooling, rather than the 6:00 start they suggested? Ask them what they would need to see in writing.
- Dr Brandt: her view on the thresholds, and on how many medical points the course needs.
- Corin and the sponsor: the earlier start loses the floodlit-finish idea, but you could offer the sponsor a different showpiece, such as a Saturday evening expo or 5k-style event on a permitted route.

**Assumptions:** I don't know your course layout, the local sunrise and temperature profile for late June, or whether the rail operator is flexible. If an earlier start proves impossible and the insurer insists on more, the next step would be talking to the federation about whether an April date could keep qualifying status. That is unlikely, so I would keep it as a fallback only.

You have a clear way forward that keeps what is working. Good luck with the board.
