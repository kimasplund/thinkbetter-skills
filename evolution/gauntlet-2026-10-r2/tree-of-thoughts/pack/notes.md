# Pack notes: tree-of-thoughts, round 2

**Brief 1 (city marathon heat plan).** Each of four keys rules out one proposal. k1: the 1 May qualifying window rules out the race director's mid-April move. Both the 9 °C mornings and his backing point toward April, and the window appears only in a sentence that explains why entries grew. k2: the 06:50 first train, given in parentheses, rules out the insurer's 6:00 start. k3: road closures are allowed only on Sunday mornings, which rules out the sponsor's Saturday-evening race. k4: the 2.5 m path and its 500-runner cap rule out the medical lead's towpath reroute. k1 and k2 are the ones a quick reply is likely to miss. What survives is a date from early May (inside the window and cooler than late June), a start no earlier than about 07:30, and a heat rule. The brief gives no May weather, so the reply has to infer the May date for itself. The hype and skeptical variants keep every fact, number and person and change only the tone.

**Brief 2 (heritage road bridge).** k1: the 1971 heritage listing is presented as postcard trivia, but it puts demolition consent in doubt. k2: the double-decker school buses can neither cross under a 7.5-tonne limit nor clear the 4.0 m railway arch on the diversion. The quarry tippers' trouble-free use of Cott Lane is the salient clue, and it points the wrong way. k3: the July-to-September river window means the replacement's two summers of river work cannot both happen before the March 2028 spending deadline, so the attractive €1.3M figure falls apart. The same deadline problem affects Holm Ford. k2 and k3 are the hard ones. Strengthening the existing bridge survives, and it has to use the summer 2027 window.

**Brief 3 (hospital imaging disaster recovery).** k1: restoring 420 TB over the 2 Gbps line takes about 19 to 21 days. Nimbara's comment that the line is plenty for replication is the misleading clue. k2: Brisewood supports its software only on its certified appliances, which blocks the natural fix of running the archive in the cloud. This key only appears once k1 has ruled out restoring. k3: St Brennan's shares the river and the substation that flooded in 2024, so it fails the flood scenario. k1 and k2 are the hard ones. The hosted standby survives, subject to checking that it can run Brisewood's appliances. The negative fixture is a single pace calculation (six hours over 42.195 km is about 8:32 per km).
