**Recommendation: keep the last Sunday of June, but start much earlier than 8:30, with the marathon first and the half later. Back it with a formal heat protocol. This is the insurer's idea, adjusted so your runners can reach the start.**

A straight 6:00 start does not work as proposed. The first Sunday train reaches Aldmere Central at 06:50, and about three-quarters of runners come by train. So the earliest start most people can make is about 07:15 to 07:30, and the heat benefit shrinks. The fix is to get an earlier train, not to give up the idea.

**Why the others lose**

- **Mid-April:** It falls outside the 1 May to 30 September qualifying window. Most of your marathon growth is club runners chasing a qualifying time, so you would likely lose the entries that drive your income. It is the best option for temperature and the worst for the Trust.
- **Saturday evening:** The city allows full road closures only on Sunday mornings, so you would need a new permit from a policy that does not offer one. At 19:00 in late June it is still warm, and the six-hour cut-off would put walkers on the course in the dark until about 01:00. The sponsor's floodlit finish does not outweigh that.
- **Towpath reroute:** The path is 2.5 m wide and the navigation trust caps a 5k there at 500 runners. It cannot carry 8,400 marathoners, and ambulances would struggle to reach a casualty in the middle 20 km. Shade is the right instinct, but this route cannot hold the field.
- **Cooling stations and a cancellation rule on their own:** More water does not lower the air temperature, and cancelling on the day wastes 13,400 entries and travel. Both are worth having, but they do not satisfy an insurer on their own.
- **Half only:** It cuts the marathon, your growth line, and the revenue behind your fixed costs. It also does not remove the heat problem, because half runners would still start at 8:30 into rising temperatures.

**Steps**

1. **Ask the train operator now for a special early Sunday service.** The target is arrival around 05:45 to 06:00 for a 06:30 marathon start. Include a return plan. Ask the city to confirm that closing roads from 05:00 covers a 06:30 start, which it should.
2. **Stagger the starts.** Run the marathon at 06:30, or at 07:15 if no early train is possible. Start the half later, around 08:00 to 08:30, so the two fields do not crowd the same stations and the medical teams. A 06:30 start with a six-hour cut-off closes the course at 12:30, well inside the 14:30 closure.
3. **Build the heat protocol with Dr Brandt and the insurer.** Use a wet-bulb or flag system with set thresholds. Include a decision time the evening before and again at 05:00. Set the actions for each level, such as a delayed start, a shortened cut-off, or stopping the course. Put the thresholds in writing for the insurer.
4. **Add cooling where it helps.** Put extra water and ice stations and shade in the last third of the marathon, where slower runners hit the heat. Put medical posts near the finish and at the towpath-adjacent shaded sections.
5. **Take a smaller towpath role if wanted.** For example, a short shaded stretch for walkers or a small relay. Do not move the main route.
6. **Tell runners early.** The earlier start is part of the entry information, with advice on travel and a warm-up.

**Assumptions and what would change this**

- **An early train is obtainable.** If the operator will not run one, a start around 07:15 to 07:30 is still better than 8:30, but the insurer may want more. Cheapest check: email the operator this week.
- **The insurer will accept an earlier start plus a written protocol.** If it insists on 6:00 or on April, you would need to weigh a loss of qualifying status against losing cover. Cheapest check: send the insurer the draft plan and ask for a yes or no before 18 November.
- **The city will allow the earlier start and staggered finish within the 05:00 to 14:30 closure.** Cheapest check: ask the events office.
- **Qualifying value drives marathon entries.** If a survey of entrants shows that most do not need a qualifier, April becomes much more attractive. Cheapest check: a short question to the existing entry list.

My confidence is moderate to high. It depends mostly on whether the operator will run an early train, since without one the gain from an earlier start is smaller.
