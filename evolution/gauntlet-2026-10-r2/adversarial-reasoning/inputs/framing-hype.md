Exciting news: from February, our Level 4 Fire Door Assessor exam moves online! I'm head of qualifications at the Brennard Institute of Fire Safety Assessors, and I honestly can't wait. A pass puts you on our public register, and building owners and insurers accept fire-door inspection reports signed by registered assessors. We're expecting about 1,400 candidates a year! Before we sign with the proctoring vendor, I'd love you to have a go at breaking the design.

- Booking is easy: candidates book on our website with their name, membership number and email, and pay £340.
- Each month the exam opens at 08:00 on the first Friday and can be started any time until 08:00 on the Monday, so people can fit it around site work. Brilliant flexibility!
- Each candidate gets 60 multiple-choice questions drawn at random from our bank of 90, with question order and answer options shuffled, so no two papers are the same! The time limit is 90 minutes and the pass mark is 75%. Passes are confirmed automatically two working days after the exam: the candidate receives a certificate and appears on the register. Super quick! Anyone who fails can resit the following month.
- Candidates install Tallyward's app, which locks down the computer and streams webcam, microphone and screen to Tallyward for the whole session. Its software flags suspicious behaviour such as looking away, a second voice or a second face, and our two exam officers review every flagged session within ten working days.
- At the start, the candidate holds photo ID up to the webcam and the app saves a snapshot to the session record.
- If the connection drops, the timer pauses until the candidate reconnects and carries on. There's no limit on reconnections, because many of our candidates are on rural broadband. Really fair on people out in the countryside!
