Change it. It's not overkill, and it's cheap to do.

Why it's worth it:
- A former dealer is the person most likely to have a reason to look, because your price lists are exactly what a competitor or his new supplier would want to see.
- The password is shared, so you can't tell who is using it. If he logs in next month, nothing will show it was him.
- The cost is one email to your remaining dealers. The cost of not changing it is that your current pricing stays visible to someone you no longer have a relationship with.

How to do it without much pain:
1. Set a new, long, random password (a passphrase of 4 or more random words works well).
2. Send it to your active dealers through email or phone, not on the download page itself. Do it the same day you change it so nobody is locked out for long.
3. Check whether the page is also reachable some other way, such as an old direct link to a PDF, a cached link in a past email, or a separate FTP or portal login. A password change won't help if the files themselves can be opened by URL without logging in.
4. If your platform allows it, check the access logs for logins since you ended the relationship, in case he already used it.

A better setup for later: give each dealer their own login, so removing one dealer takes a minute and doesn't affect anyone else. If your site software can't do that, rotate the shared password whenever a dealer leaves, and also on a regular schedule, such as once or twice a year.

One caveat: if the price lists you posted are already stale or he already had copies, changing the password won't undo that. It only stops access to what you post from now on. That's still the point of changing it.
