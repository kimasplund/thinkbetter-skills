Change it. It's not overkill, and it's about a ten-minute job.

Why it's worth doing:
- A former dealer who knows the password can read your current price lists, including any dealer-only pricing, for as long as it stays the same. He may also be talking to a competitor or to a dealer you've just replaced him with.
- Because the password is shared, you can't tell his visits from your other dealers' visits, so you'd never notice him using it.
- Anything you post from now on is visible to him too.

How to do it without a fuss:
1. Pick a new long, random password. Use a password manager to generate it.
2. Change it on the page, and check whether the old one is also stored anywhere else, such as a hosting control panel or a cached or bookmarked link.
3. Email your active dealers the new password in one message, ideally with a short note that you update it periodically. That way the change reads as routine and not as a comment on anyone.
4. If the page can give each dealer their own login, do that now or soon. Then the next time you part ways with a dealer, you only switch off one account, and you can see who is downloading what. If it can't, set a reminder to rotate the shared password every six months or so, and every time a dealer leaves.

One thing to check: if price lists or manuals have been downloaded or shared outside the page, changing the password won't take those back. Prices he has already seen are out there, so if any are sensitive, consider whether they need to change as well.

I'm assuming the page is protected only by that one shared password, with no other access control, and that the separation wasn't amicable enough to make you comfortable leaving it. If both are right, change it today.
