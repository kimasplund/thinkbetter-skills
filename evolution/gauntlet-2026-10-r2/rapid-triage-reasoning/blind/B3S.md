**Short version: unless the report is independently confirmed by about 22:35, take the splash off the front page and swap in the held feature. Holding costs you a scoop at risk. Running costs you a possible libel action and 38,000 copies you can't recall.**

**Why the default is hold**

- The plates are the only irreversible step. Everything else (web, app alert, wire) can be undone or delayed. If you are wrong, a Gazette scoop is a bad night. A forged document on the front page of a care-home fire-safety story is a legal and credibility problem for years.
- Right now the only evidence the report is genuine is a scan from one source. Vosk's lawyer has now said, specifically and in writing, that it is forged. That doesn't make it a forgery, but it is exactly the point at which you need a second route to the truth.
- The swap takes about 20 minutes. To be safe, the go/no-go has to be made by **22:35** and the subs told to start no later than **22:40**. Tell them now to have the feature ready to go, so that holding is a button press.

**What to do, in this order**

1. **Now (22:05 to 22:10): get the subs ready and try Ines again.** Tell the subs to stage the held feature. Text and call Ines, then call whoever is her deputy or the managing editor, and the paper's legal adviser or out-of-hours legal line (many groups have one). Leave a short message saying what you need: a go/no-go on the Vosk splash by 22:35. You shouldn't carry this alone, and if anyone senior picks up, the decision is theirs.
2. **22:10 to 22:30: Dagny re-verifies through a second route.** Ask her to go back to the source tonight. She should ask how the source got the report, and whether there is a second person who can confirm it exists. She should also try to reach at least one of the officers whose initials are on the routing stamp, or anyone else at the county who can confirm that the inspection happened and what it found, and look for any other independent record of that sign-off (fire service, the care home operator, the care regulator). One independent confirmation of the report's existence and findings is a reasonable bar. "Source says it's real" is not.
3. **Do not send the scan to Vosk's lawyer.** The routing stamp and officers' initials may be enough to identify your source. Sending it to the party accused of faking it would expose the source and hand the lawyer a chance to brief against it. Instead, reply by email now (a few lines, nothing more) saying you will put the allegations to Vosk formally, and ask for a specific response on the record: does Vosk dispute the findings, and on what basis? Ask which details of the report he says are false. If he will say what is false without seeing the scan, that is useful. Say you will consider anything he sends before publication. Keep this email on file.
4. **Check the document itself.** Ask Dagny or a colleague with the scan to compare it against any known genuine building-control report: layout, reference number format, officer names and whether they work there, dates, and whether the stamp matches the office's usual style. A quick check might catch an obvious problem. Don't count on it to prove authenticity.
5. **22:35 decision.**
   - **Run only if** you have independent confirmation (step 2), the document checks out in step 4, and either Ines or the legal adviser has said go. Make sure Vosk's response is in the story, and that Dagny's confirmation is noted on file.
   - **Otherwise, swap.** Don't try to run a softer version of the same splash at 22:50. A rushed rewrite under pressure is the likeliest way to end up with a legally weak story. If you have senior sign-off and a confirmed fact that stands on its own (for example, the inspection happened and a sign-off is missing), you could run that narrower version, but only with sign-off.

**Close the other doors, whichever way you go**

- **Web and app alert:** pull the 00:01 scheduled publication and the push alert now, not at 23:55. It's one switch to turn back on, and 70,000 phones can't be recalled. Re-enable it only once the story is cleared.
- **Group wire:** message the desks of the three sister titles now: "Vosk splash is on hold, do not publish at 00:01, more to follow." They are bound by the embargo, but they will act on their own copy unless told otherwise. If you run the story, tell them that too.
- **Gazette:** if they run first on something unconfirmed, that's their risk. If the report is real, it will still be real at 08:30 when building control opens, and you will have a verified version with their on-the-record response, which is stronger than a first one. Don't let the fear of being beaten be the reason to run.

**Write it down as you go**

Keep a short log: who you called, when, what was said, and why you made the final call. If you hold, the first thing tomorrow is the 08:30 call to the building control press office, with Dagny pursuing the source and the officers. If you run, the log is your evidence of the checks you made.
