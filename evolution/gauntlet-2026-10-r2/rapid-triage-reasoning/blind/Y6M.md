Short version: unless the report is independently confirmed as genuine by 22:30, swap the splash for the held-over feature and hold the story on every channel, not just print. Run it once it's confirmed, probably tomorrow morning after 08:30. Waiting costs you a possible scoop. Running a forged report costs a libel claim and a retraction, and you can't undo either. Pulping the run is the only other thing you can't undo.

**Hard times**
- 23:00: plates go on.
- 22:35: the latest you can start the swap. It takes about 20 minutes, so aim for 22:30 and leave yourself margin.
- 00:01: web publication, the app alert to 70,000 people and the embargo lifting on the three sister titles. Pulling the print splash does nothing about these.

**Do not send Vosk's lawyer your scan.** The routing stamp and officers' initials could identify your source. And if the document is real, you'd be giving the subject of the story a head start on discrediting it. Reply now with two lines. Say you take the forgery claim seriously. Say that to answer it fairly you need him to put it in writing, with whatever he can show without your document: the county's own record of that inspection, or the actual report he says exists. Ask for it by 22:30. His offer is a way to see your copy, but his reply is still useful. If he can't or won't produce anything, that is worth knowing.

**What to do between now and 22:30, in parallel**
1. Dagny rings her source now with one question: can they confirm independently, in their own words, that this report went through that office and that those officers handled it? Ask whether there is a second document or a second person. A leak alone can't tell you whether the scan is authentic. A source who can describe the report's reference number, date or the sign-off details unprompted is a better sign than one who just says "it's real".
2. Someone checks the scan for internal consistency: dates against the officers' shift patterns, the reference format against any earlier genuine county report you already hold, and the stamp style against previous routed documents. That is a quick comparison, not a forensic exam.
3. Try the county out of hours. There may be a duty officer or an emergency press line on their website. Ask only whether a report with this reference exists, not what it says. If you get a straight answer, it is the best evidence you will get tonight.
4. Get a second decision-maker. You don't need Ines's blessing, but you do need someone with authority. Call or text the deputy editor, the managing editor, or your legal adviser or duty lawyer. Message Ines once as well. If she doesn't answer, the decision is yours and you should record that you tried.

**Decision at 22:30**

Add Vosk's denial and the lawyer's forgery claim to the story, with an honest line that he was given a chance to respond. Don't claim he "faked" anything beyond what the report supports.
