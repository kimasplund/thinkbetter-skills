# Handover for Tobias: water-damaged survey maps

## Where things stand
A sprinkler head failed over the map store nine days ago and soaked 212 hand-coloured survey maps (1790s to 1840s). All 212 are in the chest freezers at -20 °C, so they are stable for now. Your job is to choose a drying method and contract it.

## Recommendation
1. **Use Nordlicht Recovery (offsite vacuum freeze-drying) for our own 151 maps.** It is the only offsite option left that the conservator accepts.
2. **Start with a test run of 2 of the least significant maps.** Dr Aino Pell, the freelance conservator, thinks freeze-drying is probably fine for the verdigris green but wants this test first. Her sign-off should come before you commit the other 149.
3. **Keep the 61 Brekke maps frozen** until the Brekke executor gives written consent. Details are below.
4. **Do not go back to Kestrel,** even at the new price (see below).

## The facts you need

**What the maps are made of.** Pell examined samples. The watercolour washes are in gum arabic, and the boundaries use verdigris green. Both are water-soluble or sensitive to moisture and heat.

**Ownership.** 151 maps are ours outright. 61 are the Brekke deposit, held under a 2009 loan agreement. Clause 7 says: "Deposited items shall not be removed from the Archive premises for any purpose without the prior written consent of the Lender." Any offsite drying of those 61 therefore needs the Lender's written consent first. The Brekke executor can only be reached through their solicitor. I have not yet contacted them about this.

**Options.**

| Option | Cost | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Nordlicht Recovery, offsite vacuum freeze-drying | €38 per map plus €420 flat transport | Preferred |
| Kestrel Document Rescue, offsite vacuum thermal drying | €19 per map (offered €13.30 now) | Rejected |
| Air-drying in-house on interleaving | Staff time, no vendor fee | Possible fallback. Table space for about 25 maps at a time, about 3 days per batch |
| Keep everything frozen until next fiscal year | Nothing now | Holding position only |

**Why Kestrel is out.** Their process thaws the items and heats them to 40 to 60 °C. Pell says that is fine for office paper but will bleed and cockle water-soluble media, and these maps are exactly that. Yesterday Kestrel rang offering 30% off, which brings the price to €13.30 a map. The price is not what ruled them out. The discount changes nothing about the process, so I would not reopen it unless Pell says otherwise. If you want to look at them again, ask her first.

**Money.**
- Valtera Mutual's adjuster will reimburse drying costs up to €6,000 in total, from any accredited vendor.
- Our remaining conservation budget this year is €1,100.
- That gives €7,100 available in total.

So our own maps fit the money. Adding the Brekke maps does not, unless something else covers the gap. The Brekke maps alone would add 61 × €38 = €2,318 (assuming one shared transport charge).

## Decisions and actions for you

**This week**
1. **Ask Pell to confirm the test-run plan** and pick the 2 least significant maps. Choose them from our own 151, not the Brekke deposit.
2. **Ask Nordlicht** (a) whether they will run a 2-map test and what it costs, in particular whether the €420 transport applies again to the main batch, (b) whether they are accredited as Valtera requires, and (c) their turnaround time and how they take frozen items.
3. **Ask the Valtera adjuster** to confirm in writing that Nordlicht qualifies, whether transport and the test run count towards the €6,000, and whether the cover includes the Brekke maps, which belong to the Lender. Also ask whether they pay the vendor directly or reimburse us afterwards, because that affects cash flow.
4. **Write to the Brekke solicitor** to explain the damage and ask for written consent under clause 7 to send the 61 maps offsite for freeze-drying. Do this early, since the executor is slow to reach.

**After the test run**
5. If Pell is happy with the two test maps, contract Nordlicht for the remaining 149 of ours.
6. Brekke maps: if consent arrives, you need a funding answer for about €2,318 (see open questions). If consent is refused or delayed, they stay frozen, or you dry them in-house as below.

## If the Brekke maps cannot go offsite
Clause 7 stops them leaving the building. The in-house route is air-drying on interleaving: 61 maps is about 3 batches of 25 at roughly 3 days each, so about 9 days of table space once started. I have not asked Pell whether she is willing to supervise air-drying of gum arabic and verdigris, and I do not know how those media behave when air-dried. That needs her view before anyone starts. Until then, leave them frozen.

## Open questions (these could change the plan)
- Will Pell sign off on freeze-drying after the test? Her current view is "probably fine" for the verdigris only.
- Will the Brekke Lender consent, and how soon?
- Who pays for the Brekke maps? The money for all 212 falls €1,376 short. Options to raise with Valtera or the solicitor are whether the Lender's own insurance covers their items, or whether Valtera's cover extends to deposited items.
- Is the €6,000 cover per claim or per item, and does it include transport? I only have the adjuster's verbal figure of "up to €6,000 total".
- When does the next fiscal year start? "Keep everything frozen until next fiscal year" is only an option if the wait is short and the freezers can safely hold 212 maps. I have not checked either. Freezing is a pause, not a solution.
- Pell's opinion on air-drying has not been asked.

## Assumptions I made while writing this
- The €420 Nordlicht transport charge is a single charge for one batch, and a separate test run may trigger a second charge.
- All 212 maps carry the same watercolour and gum arabic media. Pell tested samples, not every map.
- The insurer's accreditation requirement is met by Nordlicht. I have not seen this confirmed.
