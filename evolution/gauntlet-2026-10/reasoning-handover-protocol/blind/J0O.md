# Handover: water-damaged survey maps (for Tobias)

## Situation
- Nine days ago a sprinkler head failed over the map store. 212 hand-coloured survey maps (1790s-1840s) were soaked. All are now in our chest freezers at -20 °C, so the situation is stable and nothing is deteriorating while they stay frozen.
- Your job is to choose and contract a drying method.
- The maps fall into two groups:
  - **151 maps are ours outright.**
  - **61 maps are the Brekke deposit** (2009 loan agreement).

## The constraint that shapes everything
Clause 7 of the Brekke loan agreement says: "Deposited items shall not be removed from the Archive premises for any purpose without the prior written consent of the Lender."

- Any offsite vendor (Nordlicht, Kestrel) means removing items, so the 61 Brekke maps cannot go without written consent.
- The Brekke executor can only be reached through their solicitor. Start that contact immediately, because it will be the slowest step.
- Do not ship any Brekke map before consent is in writing.
- Also check whether the agreement requires us to notify the Lender of the damage. It probably does, so the solicitor contact should cover both.

## Options

| Option | Cost | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Nordlicht Recovery, offsite vacuum freeze-drying | €38 per map + €420 flat transport | Viable. Conservator thinks it is probably fine for the media. |
| Kestrel Document Rescue, offsite vacuum thermal drying | €19 per map (now quoted €13.30 after a 30% discount) | Ruled out (see below). |
| Air-dry in-house on interleaving | Staff time and materials | Possible. Space for about 25 maps at a time, about 3 days per batch. |
| Keep everything frozen until next fiscal year | None now | Fallback only. Buys time but does not solve anything. |

### Why Kestrel stays dropped
Dr Aino Pell (freelance conservator) examined samples. The media are watercolour washes in gum arabic, with verdigris green on the boundaries. Kestrel's process heats items to 40-60 °C. She says that is fine for office paper but will bleed and cockle water-soluble media.

The new 30% discount changes the price, not the process. The saving is real (about €5.70 a map), but it does not address the damage she described. I'd only reopen it if Kestrel can show a non-thermal process, or Pell reviews it and changes her view. I wouldn't expect either. Treat the discount call as a courtesy, not a reason to reopen.

- Our remaining conservation budget this year is **€1,100**.
- Combined, that gives **€7,100** of available funding.

## Recommended plan
This is my suggestion. You are free to change it.

1. **Now: contact the Brekke solicitor.** Ask for two things: (a) written consent under clause 7 to send the 61 maps offsite, and (b) acknowledgement of the damage. Offer the executor the details of the method and the conservator's advice.
2. **Pell test run first.** Have Dr Pell pick the 2 least significant maps, preferably from our 151, not the Brekke set. Send them to Nordlicht for freeze-drying and have her assess the verdigris and gum arabic washes. She thinks it is probably fine, but wants this check before we commit the rest.
3. **If the test passes, send our 151 maps to Nordlicht** (about €6,158 in total). The insurer covers €6,000 and the remaining €158 comes from our €1,100 budget.
4. **Brekke maps (61), two routes:**
   - **If consent arrives:** send them to Nordlicht too. The additional cost is 61 x €38 = €2,318, which brings the total to €8,476. That leaves a €1,376 shortfall over the combined funding. Options are to ask Valtera about raising the cap, to ask the Brekke estate to contribute, or to find other funds. Raise this early.
   - **If consent is refused or slow:** air-dry them in-house. This respects clause 7 because nothing leaves the premises. At about 25 maps per batch and 3 days per batch, 61 maps is 3 batches, about 9 working days, plus interleaving changes. Get Pell's view first on whether air-drying is safe for these media. Her comments covered heat, not slow air-drying, so don't assume. Also, thaw in small batches so the rest stay frozen.
5. **Fallback:** if consent is slow and Pell does not support air-drying, keep the Brekke maps frozen. That only works as long as the freezer space and power are secure, and it costs nothing, but the freezer is not a long-term answer.
