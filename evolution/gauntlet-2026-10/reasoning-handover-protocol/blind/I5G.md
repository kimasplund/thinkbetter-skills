Hold them at about -20 °C (-4 °F), which is a typical chest freezer setting. Freezing stops mold growth (it starts within 48 to 72 hours of wetting) and halts further water damage, and it keeps the paper stable until it can be dried properly.

Pack the records in freezer bags or wrap them in freezer paper, with the items interleaved with wax or freezer paper if they are coated, so they don't stick together as they freeze.
