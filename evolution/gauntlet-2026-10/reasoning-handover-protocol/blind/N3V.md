# Handover: drying the water-damaged survey maps

**To:** Tobias | **Situation:** 212 hand-coloured survey maps (1790s-1840s) were soaked nine days ago when a sprinkler head failed. All are in chest freezers at -20 °C, so they are stable for now. Your job is to choose a drying method and contract it.

## The short version

1. **Kestrel stays out, even at €13.30 a map.** The discount changes the price, not the problem. Kestrel's process thaws the maps and heats them to 40-60 °C. Dr Pell says that is fine for office paper but will bleed and cockle water-soluble media. These maps have gum arabic watercolour washes and verdigris boundaries. A cheap method that damages them is not a saving.
2. **The Brekke maps are the blocker.** 61 of the 212 belong to the Brekke deposit. Clause 7 of the 2009 loan agreement says they may not leave the premises "for any purpose without the prior written consent of the Lender". Sending them to any offsite vendor needs that consent first. The executor can only be reached through their solicitor, so **contact the solicitor on day one**. It is the slowest step.
3. **The realistic plan is a split.** Freeze-dry our 151 maps offsite with Nordlicht. Deal with the 61 Brekke maps according to the consent answer (see below).
4. **Test before committing.** Dr Pell thinks freeze-drying is probably fine for the verdigris, but she wants a test run on two of the least significant maps first. Book that before the full contract. Use maps we own outright, not Brekke maps, because the test maps will leave the building.

## The Brekke 61: two routes

- **If the Lender consents in writing:** include them in the Nordlicht shipment. Send the consent letter to Nordlicht and the insurer with the claim, and keep a copy in the deposit file. It saves a second round of transport costs.
- **If consent is refused or slow:** dry them in-house by air-drying on interleaving. With table space for about 25 maps at a time and about 3 days per batch, 61 maps is 3 batches, roughly 9-10 working days at best. The full 212 would be about 9 batches, nearly a month. **Ask Dr Pell before relying on this.** Nothing in my notes says she has approved air-drying for these media, and it carries its own cockling risk.

## Keeping everything frozen

The maps are safe frozen. Before using this as a way to wait, check two things. First, whether Valtera Mutual has a deadline for claims or for completing the work, and whether the €6,000 carries over into a new fiscal year. Second, whether the freezers can hold this load reliably for months. If the answers are not both clearly yes, don't wait.
