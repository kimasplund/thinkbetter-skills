Hold them at -20 °C (about -4 °F), or colder if your freezer allows.

Freezing stops mold growth and further water damage within the 48 to 72 hours it takes to start, and it holds the records stable until you can vacuum-freeze or air-dry them.

Assumption: a standard chest freezer at about -18 to -20 °C is fine. Frost-free (auto-defrost) units cycle warm and can slowly dry or damage items, so a manual-defrost chest freezer is better.
