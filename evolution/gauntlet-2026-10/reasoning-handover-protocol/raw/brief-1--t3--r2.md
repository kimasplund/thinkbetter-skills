# Handover: water-damaged survey maps (for Tobias)

## Situation
Nine days ago a sprinkler head failed over the map store. 212 hand-coloured survey maps (1790s-1840s) were soaked. All 212 are now in the chest freezers at -20 °C. Nothing is drying yet, and the frozen state is stable. Your job is to choose a drying method and contract it.

## The decision in short
Use Nordlicht Recovery (offsite vacuum freeze-drying) for the 151 maps we own, after a two-map test run. Do not use Kestrel. The 61 Brekke maps cannot go anywhere until the Brekke executor consents in writing. Start that request through their solicitor today.

## Facts you need
**Ownership**
- 151 maps are ours outright.
- 61 maps are the Brekke deposit (2009 loan agreement). Clause 7 says: "Deposited items shall not be removed from the Archive premises for any purpose without the prior written consent of the Lender."
- The Brekke executor can only be reached through their solicitor. Sending those maps to Nordlicht without written consent would breach the agreement.

**Money**
- Insurer: Valtera Mutual reimburses drying up to EUR 6,000 in total, with any accredited vendor.
- Our remaining conservation budget this year is EUR 1,100.
- Available money: EUR 7,100.

**Options and costs**

| Option | Cost | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Nordlicht, freeze-drying, offsite | EUR 38 per map + EUR 420 flat transport | Recommended for our 151 |
| Kestrel, vacuum thermal drying, offsite | EUR 19 per map, now offered at 30% off = EUR 13.30 | Ruled out (see below) |
| Air-dry in-house on interleaving | Staff time only | Possible for Brekke maps. Space for about 25 maps at a time, about 3 days per batch |
| Keep everything frozen until next fiscal year | None now | Fallback only |

**Costs worked through**
- Nordlicht, our 151 maps: 151 x 38 = EUR 5,738, plus EUR 420 transport = **EUR 6,158**. That is EUR 158 over the insurer's cap. The budget covers the gap and leaves about EUR 942.
- Nordlicht, all 212 maps: 212 x 38 = 8,056, plus 420 = **EUR 8,476**. That is EUR 1,376 more than the EUR 7,100 available. All 212 maps are not affordable this way, even before the Brekke consent problem.
- The Brekke 61 at Nordlicht would add EUR 2,318. Do not plan on that unless the money question (below) is settled.

## Why Kestrel is out, including at 30% off
Dr Aino Pell (freelance conservator) examined samples. The maps have watercolour washes in gum arabic and verdigris green on the boundaries. Kestrel's process thaws the items and heats them to 40-60 °C. She says that is fine for office paper but will bleed and cockle water-soluble media, which is what these are. We dropped Kestrel on that basis.

Kestrel rang yesterday offering 30% off (EUR 13.30 a map, about EUR 2,820 for all 212). The discount changes the price and not the process, so Pell's objection stands. I would only reopen it if Kestrel can show a process that avoids heating, and Pell approves it in writing. Be cautious about the pull of a big saving here, because the damage would be permanent.

## Recommended plan
1. **Today: contact the Brekke solicitor.** Ask for written consent from the executor for one of two things. Either (a) the 61 maps go offsite for drying, or (b) we dry them in-house. Say that the maps are frozen and stable, and ask for a reply date. Also ask whether the executor has insurance of their own on the deposit.
2. **Today: ask Valtera Mutual's adjuster three things.**
   - Does the EUR 6,000 cap cover Brekke items as well as our own?
   - Does the policy have a claim deadline or a time limit on keeping items frozen?
   - Is Nordlicht an accredited vendor? The adjuster said any accredited vendor.
3. **Book the test run with Nordlicht and Pell.** Pell thinks freeze-drying is probably fine for the verdigris but wants a test on two of the least significant maps first. Pick the two with Pell, and make sure they are ours and not Brekke. Ask Nordlicht whether a two-map test carries its own EUR 420 transport charge. If it does, the plan costs more than EUR 6,158. Tell them you want to fold the test and the main batch into one contract with a hold point after the test.
4. **If the test is good,** send the remaining 149 of our maps to Nordlicht. Total EUR 6,158. Claim EUR 6,000 from Valtera and charge EUR 158 to the conservation budget.
5. **If the test shows damage,** stop. Do not send the rest. Go back to Pell for what to do next. The fallbacks are keeping everything frozen or in-house air-drying, and I do not have a view from Pell on air-drying these particular media.
6. **Brekke maps,** depending on the answer to step 1:
   - Consent for offsite drying and extra money found (insurer cover or lender contribution): add them to the Nordlicht job.
   - No consent, or no money: either keep them frozen until the executor decides, or air-dry in-house in batches. With space for about 25 and about 3 days per batch, 61 maps is 3 batches (25, 25, 11), so roughly 9 days of table time. Ask Pell first whether in-house air-drying is safe for the washes and verdigris.

## Open questions that could change the plan
- Does the insurer's cap cover the Brekke maps, and does the policy set a deadline?
- Does the Nordlicht test run cost an extra transport fee?
- Will the executor consent, and how soon? The solicitor is the only route.
- Is in-house air-drying acceptable to Pell for these media?
- Is Nordlicht accredited with Valtera?

## What is settled and what is assumed
- **Settled:** inventory of 151 and 61, the clause 7 wording, the costs above, Pell's opinion on Kestrel's heat, and the EUR 6,000 and EUR 1,100 figures.
- **Not settled:** Pell's view on freeze-drying is "probably fine" and not confirmed. The test run is what confirms it.
- **My assumptions:** the freezers are holding at -20 °C with a monitored alarm, and there is no hard insurance deadline. Please check both, because I have not confirmed either.
