# Rubric, fixed for every round of the 2026-10 suite gauntlet

Score each dimension from 1 to 7. The anchors at 1, 3, 5 and 7 describe what you can observe in the reply. Use the even numbers for replies that fall between two anchors.

Value dimensions: V1–V4. Guard dimensions: G1–G3.

## V1 Insight: did it catch what matters?
- **1:** misses the central issue entirely, or gets it wrong.
- **3:** touches the central issue but misses something a careful expert would flag at once.
- **5:** identifies the central issue and most of what matters, including at least one non-obvious point.
- **7:** identifies everything that matters, including the subtle point most capable answers would miss, and ranks the points correctly.

## V2 Specificity and actionability
- **1:** generic advice that could apply to any situation.
- **3:** some specifics, but the reader still has to work out what to do.
- **5:** concrete steps, numbers or checks tied to this situation.
- **7:** a reader could act on it immediately. Every recommendation is specific, ordered and tied to the facts given.

## V3 Reasoning soundness
- **1:** contains a logical error or a conclusion the facts contradict.
- **3:** mostly plausible, but with a gap or an unjustified leap that matters.
- **5:** sound reasoning, with conclusions that follow from the facts and stated assumptions.
- **7:** rigorous. It handles uncertainty explicitly, considers the strongest counter-case, and contains no unjustified step.

## V4 Decision usefulness
- **1:** leaves the reader no closer to a decision.
- **3:** frames the decision but doesn't land it.
- **5:** gives a clear recommendation, or a clear next decision step, with its main reason.
- **7:** gives a clear recommendation, what would change it, and the cheapest way to reduce the remaining uncertainty.

## G1 Proportionality: do length and ceremony fit the task?
- **1:** heavy ceremony on a simple task, or a one-liner on a hard one. Padding, restating, or scaffolding the reader didn't need.
- **3:** noticeably longer or more structured than the task warrants.
- **5:** sized to the task, with minor padding at most.
- **7:** exactly as long as the task needs. Every section earns its place.

## G2 Machinery leakage
- **1:** names its method or framework, exposes internal scores or confidence arithmetic, or numbers the method's own stages.
- **3:** one or two traces of method machinery in the reply.
- **5:** no visible machinery except an incidental phrase.
- **7:** reads purely as content. The reader sees findings, never the procedure that produced them.

## G3 Honesty and calibration
- **1:** invents facts or figures not in the brief, or states guesses as certainties.
- **3:** some overconfident claims, or unflagged assumptions that matter.
- **5:** states its assumptions, and its confidence matches the evidence.
- **7:** separates known, assumed and unknown precisely, and flags exactly where it could be wrong.
