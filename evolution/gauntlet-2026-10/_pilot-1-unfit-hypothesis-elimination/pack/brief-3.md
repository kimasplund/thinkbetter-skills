I run growth at Tunewright, a practice app for adult music learners. We get about 9,000 free-trial starts a month across iOS, Android and web; Android is roughly 60% of trials. Trial-to-paid conversion for trials started September 1 to 21 came in at 29%. For the previous six months it held between 39% and 42%. The board meets in two weeks and the CEO wants a cause, not a guess.

What changed:
- Aug 28: price went from $8.99 to $10.99 a month in the US, UK and EU. Brazil, India and Mexico stayed at their existing regional prices.
- Aug 30: we started a 50/50 test of a new onboarding flow. Day-3 practice rate is lower in the new flow (52% vs 58% in control).
- Aug 31: Android 5.2 shipped: crash fixes plus the billing library upgrade the store now requires.
- Sept 3: iOS 7.4 shipped with a new tuner feature.

What we see:
- Conversion by region: US/UK/EU went from 41% to 31%; Brazil/India/Mexico went from 33% to 20%.
- Conversion by onboarding arm: control 29.4%, new flow 28.7%.
- Support tickets tagged "billing" went from about 12 a week to about 50. Our support lead says most of them are people unhappy about the price.
- Refunds in the Play Console jumped from about 20 a week to about 300 a week starting the first week of September. App Store refunds are flat. Our PM reads that as people trying the paid plan and asking for their money back after the price rise.

The CEO wants to roll the price back and kill the new onboarding flow before the board meeting. Is that the right call? What actually happened to conversion?
