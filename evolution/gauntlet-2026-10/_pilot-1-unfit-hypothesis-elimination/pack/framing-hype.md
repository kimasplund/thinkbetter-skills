What changed this summer (busy summer, we've been levelling up!):
- July 20: tightened the mill gap (every batch since).
- July 28: fitted inline density meters to FV3 and FV4, so those controllers start the cold crash by themselves when gravity hits target. FV1 and FV2 still crash when someone takes a hydrometer reading.
- August 1: Pia joined. Great hire!
- Batch 3 was the first on our new yeast lot, L22.
- August 10: switched CIP sanitizer to a new peracetic product.
- FV3's controller sat in manual mode for most of batch 9 while the vendor pushed a firmware update.

Also: August was hot and the glycol chiller ran hard, but every tank's temperature log stayed within 0.5°C of setpoint. We plated samples from batches 7 and 8 and nothing grew, so it's definitely not an infection!

Our head brewer is certain it's the new yeast lot, and honestly the timing feels perfect. He wants to dump L22 and switch labs, about $1,800 with the downtime, which feels like a small price to get our flagship back. Pia brewed three of the five buttery batches and is quietly worried everyone thinks it's her. So: what's actually causing this, and what should we check before we spend the money? Let's get Copperline back!
