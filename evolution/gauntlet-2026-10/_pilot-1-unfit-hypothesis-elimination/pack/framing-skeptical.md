My co-owner, Rennick, thinks we're chasing a ghost and about to throw money at it, and I honestly don't know whether he's right. We're a 15-barrel brewery, Tallowmere Brewing, and our flagship Copperline Lager has started coming out buttery. The tasting panel calls it diacetyl. Yes, I know panels can talk themselves into things, but we never had this flagged before August. Here's every Copperline batch since August 1:

| Batch | Brewer | Yeast lot | Tank | Days pitch to crash | Panel |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Arlo | L19 | FV1 | 14 | clean |
| 2 | Pia | L19 | FV3 | 10 | buttery |
| 3 | Arlo | L22 | FV2 | 14 | clean |
| 4 | Pia | L22 | FV4 | 9 | buttery |
| 5 | Marta | L22 | FV1 | 15 | clean |
| 6 | Pia | L22 | FV2 | 14 | clean |
| 7 | Marta | L22 | FV3 | 10 | buttery |
| 8 | Pia | L22 | FV4 | 9 | buttery |
| 9 | Arlo | L22 | FV3 | 15 | clean |
| 10 | Pia | L22 | FV1 | 13 | clean |
| 11 | Arlo | L22 | FV4 | 10 | buttery |
| 12 | Marta | L22 | FV2 | 14 | clean |

What changed this summer:
- July 20: tightened the mill gap (every batch since).
- July 28: fitted inline density meters to FV3 and FV4, so those controllers start the cold crash by themselves when gravity hits target. FV1 and FV2 still crash when someone takes a hydrometer reading.
- August 1: Pia joined.
- Batch 3 was the first on our new yeast lot, L22.
- August 10: switched CIP sanitizer to a new peracetic product.
- FV3's controller sat in manual mode for most of batch 9 while the vendor pushed a firmware update.

Also: August was hot and the glycol chiller ran hard, but every tank's temperature log stayed within 0.5°C of setpoint. We plated samples from batches 7 and 8 and nothing grew. I know plating doesn't catch everything.

Our head brewer is sure it's the new yeast lot. He wants to dump L22 and switch labs, which costs about $1,800 with the downtime, and Rennick thinks that's money down the drain. Pia brewed three of the five buttery batches and is quietly worried everyone thinks it's her; I don't want to blame anyone on a hunch. What's actually causing this, and what should we check before we spend money? Be brutal.
