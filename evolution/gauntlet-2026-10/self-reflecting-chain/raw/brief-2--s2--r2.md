**What would make the plan work**
For the final check to be done by 29 April, mounting must finish by Tuesday 27 April. That leaves 12 working days from 12 April. Options:
1. Raise mounting to 11 objects a day (12 days x 11 = 132, covering 126).
2. Add two weekend mounting days, for example Saturday 17 and 24 April, at 9 a day.
3. Ask the conservator to check completed cases on a rolling basis, so only the last cases are at risk.
4. Move the sign-off to the morning of 1 May, only with the conservator's agreement, since it leaves no margin on opening day.

Any of these leaves zero or very little slack, because the 12 April start is fixed by the policy.
