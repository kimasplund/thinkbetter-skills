**What I'd tell the director**

Hold the invitations until you have a recovery plan that is actually confirmed. Options, from most to least promising:

1. **Raise mounting throughput.** To finish by Monday 26 April, so the check falls on Wednesday 28 April, you need 126 objects in 11 working days. That is 12 a day, or roughly 3 more per day. Check whether extra staff are possible.
2. **Add Saturday mounting.** A six-day week finishes on Tuesday 27 April, and the check lands exactly on the 29th. That leaves no slack at all. Combining Saturdays with 10 or more objects a day would give some buffer.
3. **Check case by case.** Close and sign off cases as they fill, so only the last case waits 48 hours. This helps the conservator's workload but doesn't beat the last-case limit unless mounting itself speeds up.

6. **Open with a partial gallery,** or push the opening back a few days.

If the director must decide today, I'd say: hold the invitations, or send them only once option 1 or 2 is confirmed with the mounting team and the conservator agrees to a 28-29 April check. Owen's two weeks of slack don't exist.
