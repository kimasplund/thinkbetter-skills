I'm production manager for Thistledown, a three-day music festival on farmland near Wetherholm, about 7,000 people. We're 11 weeks out, and I need to lock the power plan by the end of the month.

Every year we've rented six 200 kVA diesel generators from Gantry Power Hire at £4,200 each for the event. Last year they burned 9,800 litres of diesel at £1.70 a litre, and peak demand across the site was about 520 kW. Two of the six sit at the edge of the campsite and run the showers, lighting and medical tent. The medical contractor needs uninterrupted power to their tent all night, and overnight the campsite as a whole draws about 45 kW.

This year Gantry is pushing hybrid sets: a 100 kVA generator paired with a 120 kWh battery pack, up to 180 kW with both running, at £5,100 each. They say six hybrids would cover the same peaks and cut fuel by about 55%, because the engine shuts off whenever the battery can carry the load. I'm wary of paying more per unit for kit we've never used.

What I'd really like is the grid. The landowner, Gwen Aske, has offered to go halves on a permanent connection, since we have a ten-year lease. The network operator quoted £38,000 for a 400 A three-phase supply to the field, with a 14-week lead time. After that, no generators and no fuel, ever.

One more thing: the council renewed our licence this year with a new condition, after last year's noise complaints. No generator within 150 m of the campsite may run between 23:00 and 07:00.

How should we power the festival?
