My call: don't cancel, and don't wait for the X-ray. Plan to open tonight with Liv, with the lift out of use, and tell the audience and critics now. Both options on the table rest on the wrong question.

**Why not wait for Signe's X-ray (director's option)**
- The X-ray is around 19:00, which is when doors open. Even a clear result only tells you about bone. It says nothing about whether she can do a fight with Edvin on a sprain, and she hasn't rehearsed on it.
- Waiting also leaves the real hazard unsolved. The lift dropped 40 cm without warning, and nobody can inspect it until tomorrow. That is true whether Signe or Liv goes on.

**Why not cancel now (duty manager's option)**
- It's irreversible. Refunds go out automatically and the seats are released, so you can't undo it if it turns out you could have gone on.
- You have a way to run the show: a prepared understudy and a venue that is otherwise fine. The only thing you've lost is the lift.
- Cancelling a press night with 540 sold costs you the reviews and the goodwill, and it doesn't reduce the safety risk any more than simply not using the lift does.

**What to do, in order**
1. **Now, 18:10 to 18:20: take the lift out of service.** Isolate the power if you can, or barrier and tape it. Tell the stage manager and the duty manager. Nobody uses it tonight, Liv included.
2. **Ask the stage manager and director one question: can the Act 2 entrance be staged without the lift?** For example, a walk-on from the wings or a changed reveal. If yes, you go. If the show truly can't work without the lift, that is the one case where cancelling or postponing is right. I'd expect a workaround to exist, because ten minutes of re-blocking is cheaper than losing the night.
3. **Confirm Liv is happy to go on.** She has done the put-in and the half-speed fight. Run the Act 2 fight with Edvin at a safe pace and agree any simplifications before curtain. Do not let it run at full speed untested.
4. **Decide by about 18:30 and then communicate.**
   - Ask the arts centre to put a notice on the website, ticketing and the door that the lead role will be played by Liv tonight, and that you are offering a refund or exchange to anyone who prefers one. Because most of the audience is local, that message reaches people before they set off.
   - Don't use the "cancel performance" button for this. Handle refunds individually.
5. **Tell the six critics directly.** Say there has been a cast change due to an injury, give Liv's name, and offer a return visit to see Signe once she is back. Many critics will accept that and some will prefer it. Do this yourself by phone or email, not through the notice alone.
6. **Timing buffer.** The show is 2h15 and the curfew is 22:30. Starting at 19:30 ends about 21:45, which leaves room. A late start of up to about 20:00 still ends around 22:15, but that is too tight to count on. Treat 19:45 as the latest start.
7. **Signe.** Tell her tonight is covered and her job is to get the ankle assessed properly. If she is cleared she can return when a safe fight can be rehearsed and the lift has been inspected. Tell her about the lift decision so she isn't hearing it second-hand.
8. **Log it.** Write down the incident, the time, who was told, and the decision to take the lift out of use. Report it to the venue so the lift is inspected before anyone uses it again.

**Fallback.** If by 18:30 you can't make the entrance work without the lift, or Liv or the stage manager says the show isn't safe, then cancel, but do it deliberately and early, with a message to the audience and a rescheduled date. It should be the fallback, not the default.

**Assumptions.** I'm assuming Liv is available and willing, the venue allows the lift to be isolated, and the stage manager can re-block one entrance. If any of those is false, go to the fallback.
