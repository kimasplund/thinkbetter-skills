# Pack notes: rapid-triage-reasoning

**Brief 1, theatre press night with an injured lead.** Each key pairs an irreversible step with a reversible one that buys time. k1 sets the cancel-and-refund button against going on with the understudy. It's the visible decision, and most replies should catch it. k2 is hidden behind the director's sensible-sounding "put her on if it's clear": the X-ray is the salient clue and points the wrong way, because it can't clear a sprain. k3 sits in a one-line aside about the technician. It only shows up once the reply stops treating the evening as a casting question: the faulty lift is still in tonight's Act 2 entrance for whoever plays the role. k4 needs the reader to separate "tonight's performance" from "press night". Press night can be moved, but published reviews can't be withdrawn. The hype and skeptical framings keep every fact, number and preference unchanged.

**Brief 2, ransomware at an accounting practice.** k1 and k2 are the decisive, easily missed keys. Both come from following the contractor's confident instruction to leave the server running. In k1, the 19:00 NAS job fires before he arrives and overwrites the last clean local copy. k2 takes arithmetic: 380 GB of encrypted re-uploads against about 160 GB of free quota triggers the cloud's trimming of old versions, which are the clean ones. k3 sets the overnight wipe and reinstall against isolating and preserving the server: the wipe destroys breach evidence and breaks the insurer's notify-first condition. k4 sets paying tonight against deferring. The ransom clock is 72 hours, and the VAT deadline is a false clock that can be relieved without an irreversible step.

**Brief 3, dairy farm with treated milk in the bulk tank.** k1, refusing the tanker and rejecting the dilution argument, is the obvious key. k2 and k3 both sit inside the apparently safe "just dump it" option. Dumping before a lab result forfeits the insurance claim. The habitual disposal route, the yard drain into the beck, is itself an irreversible pollution incident, and the precedent makes it look normal. k4 only becomes visible after the reply has ruled out shipping and dumping and decided to hold the tank. Holding means the 16:00 milking can't go into it, on both capacity (14,500 against 13,000 litres) and contamination grounds, so the spare tank has to be readied and the cow re-marked before then.
