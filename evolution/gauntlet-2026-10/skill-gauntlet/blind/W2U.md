Here is each of your four criteria in turn.

**1. Higher actionability: not established.**
v9 averages 5.6 against 5.2 for v8, a gap of 0.4 on a 7-point scale. Individual runs overlap heavily (v9 4.5–6.6, v8 4.4–6.1). With 10 questions, 3 runs each and 3 judges, a gap that small could easily be noise. The direction favors v9, but you can't call it "higher" with confidence. A paired comparison per question would settle it: for each of the 10 questions, take v9's mean minus v8's mean, then check how many questions favor v9 and whether the differences are consistently positive.

**2. Not lower on concision or safety: probably met, but the concision result is compromised.**

A pesticide recommendation that flips with the asker's mood is a safety problem, not a style issue. It also suggests v9's clarifying question isn't doing its job: if it had gathered crop stage and scouting counts, the advice should not depend on tone.

I'd also want to know whether this is one question or a pattern. You only report one tone pair. If that is the only pair you ran, the criterion is under-tested. If you ran more, report all of them.
