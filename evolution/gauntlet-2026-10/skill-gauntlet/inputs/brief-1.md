I lead client communications at Bramblecote Veterinary Group (14 clinics). After each visit our vets type shorthand notes, and a prompt we call DischargeWriter turns them into take-home instructions for owners. v2.3 is live. Dr. Mirela Achterberg rewrote it this month as v2.4: it adds a medication-schedule table and three worked examples (a missed carprofen dose, keeping the cone on overnight, soft food after dental work).

On September 2, before running anything, we wrote the rule down: v2.4 ships only if it is no worse than v2.3 on dosing accuracy and length, and beats it on owner clarity by at least 1.0 point on our 1–7 scale. We ran 12 real case notes through each version twice and relabeled the outputs A and B so nobody knew which was which. Judges were Callum Pell (senior nurse), Bea Moreno (client services) and Dr. Achterberg, since she knows the case types best.

Results came back September 9:

- Owner clarity: v2.4 ahead by 0.8.
- Dosing accuracy: the judges scored both 5.0 out of 5. Our pharmacy tech also checked every output line by line against the source notes and found two v2.4 tables where a twice-daily drug was written as once daily; none in v2.3. The judges are the clinicians, so I'd go with their score.
- Length: about even.
- We also gave both versions one quick owner question as a sanity check: what to do about a missed carprofen dose. v2.4 answered in two sentences; v2.3 produced a full discharge sheet.

On September 10 we corrected the clarity bar to 0.5. The 1.0 was carried over from our old 10-point scale and was always too high for a 7-point one.

So v2.4 clears everything. Can I tell the clinics it goes live Monday?
