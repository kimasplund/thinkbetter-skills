Notes (J-A, practitioner persona, brief-2):

- C4F: Short and usable. It gets the works-vs-concerts reading and the Saturday focus right, and it suggests pulling Saturday results by programme type. It invents "whole contemporary programmes, which are the weakest sellers in the main hall", which the brief does not support.
- H9Z: Sound and well hedged, with a good list of what could go wrong (Unbound may not scale, short openers may be resented). It runs long and says some things twice. The "one piece in 12 of 16 concerts" figure only holds at about 56 works.
- P5I: Strong slot-level decision rules and a 60% review trigger. Good ideas on co-funding commissions and on asking whether the principals' concern is about ambition or about pay and workload. Calibrated about its confidence.
- E2V: Compact and sensible. It states its assumptions and raises guest-fee net effects. It is thinner on what would change the recommendation, and it overstates the film concert as "not a one-off to dismiss".
- M1S: Catches subtle costs others miss: rights fees for film and game scores, rehearsal time, and Unbound's roughly 1,000-seat total capacity. It asks the council about proof and tolerance, and its next step compares all three plans on the same measures.
- C5N: The most actionable before-March list: count last season's living-composer works, have the council review a draft, and meet the principals first. It also splits decision ownership between Gerrit and Ilse. It is long, and it names real composers as examples, which is useful but adds unverified detail.
- Z6X: The best "what would change my view" section, with three concrete triggers. It suggests pulling per-concert sales by repertoire type as the cheap test, and it makes the differentiation argument against greatest hits. It assumes without saying so that €70k is small relative to the budget.
- C3U: A detailed plan with good measurement, but it invents a fact: that the decline "came while you were programming mostly familiar work". It also calls 12 a "safe target" when up to 13 may be required.
