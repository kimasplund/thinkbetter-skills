Yes, it's worth separating them, and your room is big enough to do it. Put cats on one long wall and dogs on the other. That leaves about 5 metres between the rows, and the chairs themselves don't need to move.

Keep the cats' side as calm as you can. Ask owners to keep cats in carriers and set the carriers on the chair or on their lap, not the floor. Keep dogs leashed. Give anxious or reactive pets the option to wait in the car or in a free exam room.

Assumption: I'm taking your 5 by 6 m figures as the floor size. If the two rows are only a metre or two apart, the split helps less, so the carrier and leash rules matter more.
