## What to change in the Monday blitz

1. **Don't send the current English files to the translators as-is.** Rulebook v2.1 isn't done until the end of next week. It folds Exhaust into Rest and adds two Relic cards. Four translators working from v2.0 would translate the wrong keyword in every card and rulebook passage that mentions Exhaust. They would also miss the two new cards. Fixing that means four rounds of rework, and one of them goes through Casa Tabuleiro's sign-off. Start them on the stable part instead (see the plan below).
2. **Don't have Tomasz build four full layouts from the English files.** Your wordiest English cards already fill the fixed text box, and German, Polish and Portuguese usually run longer than English. Layouts built around English would overflow once the real text drops in, and they would also need redoing for v2.1. His Monday work is to fix the template and test it with stand-in text.
3. **Don't ask Jonas for a quote on 220 cards per edition.** With the two new Relics you will have 222 unique cards. Veldt prints on sheets of 110, so 220 cards is 2 sheets per edition and 222 is 3. That is 12 sheets across four editions instead of 8, and the third sheet is almost empty. Finance should approve the budget for the real number.

## Risks to watch

- **The schedule has no slack.** The Brazilian edition goes to Casa at the end of week 5 and their approval ends the Friday before Veldt's deadline. If their review overruns, or layout of any edition slips, the Brazilian edition misses the printer slot. Two things would help:
  - Ask Casa now whether they will accept the translated text for an early read before the final PDFs.
  - Ask Veldt whether the Brazilian edition can follow a few days after the other three.
- **This plan assumes** each translator can start on part of the material and finish the rest in about three weeks overall. If a translator needs all the v2.1 material before starting, translation moves later, and the Brazilian edition can't meet the deadline. Check this with all four on Monday.
- **Moving the freeze earlier** would help everything. If Priya can lock the Exhaust-to-Rest wording and the two Relic cards by the end of week 1, the translators can start on the full set sooner and slack returns to the plan.

The parallel work that is safe on Monday is the translators starting on stable cards, Tomasz stress-testing the template, and Jonas getting the quote. The rest has to wait for v2.1.
