## Three changes to the Monday plan

**1. Don't send the current English files to the translators yet.**
Priya's v2.1 changes the content, not only the wording. Folding Exhaust into Rest changes a keyword that appears on cards and in the rulebook, and the two Relic cards make the set 222. If translators start on today's files, they will translate a keyword that is going away. You would pay for that work twice, and the four translations could end up inconsistent. Translation should start from v2.1 once it is frozen. That is one week later than you wanted, and the plan below still fits.

**2. Tomasz shouldn't build final layouts from the English files.**
The rules-text box is fixed, and your wordiest English cards already fill it. German and Polish usually run noticeably longer than English, perhaps 20 to 30 percent, and Portuguese also tends to be longer. If he lays out from English, the text will overflow once translations drop in, and you will find that in week 5, when there is no time left. This week he should instead:
- Build the four edition templates and the rulebook layouts.
- Stress-test the card template with padded dummy text, using your wordiest cards at about +30 percent.
- Decide how overflow is handled: a smaller font, a larger box, or character limits that translators must meet.

He should send the translators the box size and a per-card character budget by Friday. Translators then write to the limit from the start and don't need to cut text afterwards.

**3. Jonas should ask Veldt to quote 222 cards, not 220.**
Veldt prints uncut sheets of 110 and quotes per sheet. 220 cards is exactly 2 sheets per edition, or 8 sheets in total. With the two new Relics you have 222 cards, which means 3 sheets per edition, or 12 sheets. That is a 50 percent jump in print cost for two cards. Jonas should ask Veldt for both quotes (8 sheets and 12 sheets). He should also ask what could go on the spare slots of a third sheet, such as extra reference or token cards, since you would be paying for them anyway. Finance should approve against the 12-sheet figure, with the 8-sheet figure as the best case if you decide to cut back to 220. Please confirm with Priya the final card count. If Exhaust disappearing removes a card, the numbers change.
