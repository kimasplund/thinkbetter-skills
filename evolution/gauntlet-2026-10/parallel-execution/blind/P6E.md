## What to change in the Monday plan

**2. Don't build final layouts from the English files.** The rules-text box is fixed, and your wordiest English cards already fill it. German and Polish usually run 20-35% longer than English, so those cards will overflow. Tomasz building four layouts from English would be building on a template that probably fails. - Stress-test the template with pseudo-localized text, meaning the longest cards padded by about 35%, in German and Polish.

Then each edition gets built from the corrected template as translations come in.
