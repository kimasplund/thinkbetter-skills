# Pack notes: analogical-transfer

**Brief 1 (repertory theatre membership, gym analogy).** The tempting parallel is the gym or streaming flat fee. Four disanalogies break it. k1, the obvious one: sold-out Friday and Saturday seats are perishable inventory, so member admissions displace €34 sales. k2, subtle and buried in the licensing bullet: zero-price admissions count as complimentary, which caps them at 4% of the house (about 20 seats per performance), and gyms pay no per-admission royalties. k3, subtle: a theatre booking reserves a scarce seat, and the 14% comp no-show rate shows what free bookings do. k4, moderate: gym economics rely on breakage, but the likely buyers are the 1,100 subscribers and 1,800 frequent attenders, so the pass re-prices the core audience downward. The framing pair keeps every number and clause identical; the skeptical version pre-defends only the director's untested claim and the novelty point, so it does not point at any key.

**Brief 2 (forest disease outbreak, wildfire firebreak analogy).** The fire officer's firebreak is the surface analogy. k1, obvious: the beetle vector flies 3 km, so a 400 m contiguous-spread barrier is the wrong shape. k2, subtle: 18 to 24 months of latency, plus 6 of 20 symptom-free trees already infected, mean the visible boundary lies inside the real front, unlike a visible fire line. k3, subtle and the inverse of the analogy: felling creates the beetle's breeding material rather than removing fuel, and roadside stacking through the May to July flight season makes it worse. k4, moderate: campers carrying site-cut firewood home create long-distance jumps no perimeter can stop.

**Brief 3 (freelance subtitlers' hardship fund, union sick-club analogy).** The sick club assumes independent, unchosen, temporary shocks, and each assumption fails. k1, moderate to subtle: 31 of 46 members bottom out in January and August, so the losses are correlated and predictable rather than insurable. k2, subtle: the trigger is monthly invoiced income, which members control through invoice timing and by declining desk offers. k3, subtle: Halcyra's move to post-editing machine drafts is a permanent structural decline in half the work, which a smoothing fund cannot finance and which its trailing-average threshold quietly ratchets away. k4, the more obvious insurance point: voluntary, join-any-time enrolment invites adverse selection, because freelancers can see their own pipelines.
