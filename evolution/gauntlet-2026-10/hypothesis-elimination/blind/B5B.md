Order of draw (CLSI standard) for those four tubes:

1. **Light blue** (sodium citrate)
2. **Red or gold** (serum, clot activator / SST)
3. **Green** (heparin)
4. **Lavender** (EDTA)

Memory aid: "Blue, Red/Gold, Green, Lavender" (full order also puts sterile/blood culture bottles first and gray last).

Notes for the cheat sheet:
- The order prevents additive carryover. For example, EDTA (lavender) contaminating a later tube can falsely raise potassium and lower calcium.
- If you draw a light blue tube alone with a butterfly, draw a discard tube first so the tubing's dead space is filled with blood.
- Full CLSI sequence: blood culture, light blue, red/gold (serum), green, lavender, gray.
- Check your facility's policy, since some labs differ slightly (for example, where a plain red tube sits relative to gold).
