Correct order of draw for those four tubes (CLSI standard):

1. **Light blue** (sodium citrate)
2. **Red or gold** (serum, clot activator; gold is the gel separator tube)
3. **Green** (heparin)
4. **Lavender** (EDTA)

Why this order:
- Light blue goes first among these because coagulation tests are sensitive to contamination from other additives.
- Lavender goes after green because EDTA carried over into other tubes can falsely raise potassium and lower calcium, magnesium, and iron. It can also interfere with some coagulation and enzyme tests.

Notes for the cheat sheet:
- Blood culture bottles or tubes come before all of these. Gray (sodium fluoride/oxalate) comes last.
- The full sequence is: blood culture, light blue, red/gold, green, lavender, gray.
- If you draw only a light blue tube with a butterfly, collect a discard tube first so the tubing's air volume doesn't underfill the citrate tube.
- Some labs list a "light green" (lithium heparin gel) in the green slot, and some list a separate "orange" or "royal blue" tube. Check your facility's policy for those.
