I'm executive director of Ledgerwood Reading Partners, a nonprofit that runs one-on-one after-school reading tutoring in four elementary schools in the Fennick Unified district, about 160 kids. The Arbelle Foundation has offered us $1.2M over two years to expand to 14 schools. Our board votes on accepting it October 8. Can you poke holes in the plan before then?

- Hire 10 new site coordinators to start November 1, recruit and train tutors over November and December, and start tutoring at all 10 new schools on January 12: 40 kids per school, twice a week.
- Tutors: we have about 70, nearly all Harmon State education students earning practicum credit. We'd need roughly 175 more. Harmon's spring semester starts January 20.
- All 10 principals have told me they're excited to have us.
- Fennick requires a district-office MOU for any outside program using school space or student data. Our MOU for the current four schools took about five months to get signed, but we know the people there now.
- The expansion costs about $50k a month once coordinators start. Arbelle pays quarterly in arrears on submitted expense reports, usually 45 days after the quarter closes. Its quarters run November–January, February–April, and so on.
- We have about $150k in unrestricted reserves.
- The second-year $600k is released only if students at the new schools outscore a comparison group by at least 15 points on the district's spring reading benchmark in mid-April. At our current schools, our kids outscore comparison students by about 12 points after a full October–April year.

Success: all 14 schools running in January, and the second-year money released next summer.
