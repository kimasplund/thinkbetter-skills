Two quick checks are worth doing before you say yes:

1. **Ask the other six.** Two students asking doesn't tell you what the rest can manage. Send one message ("Starting at 10:30 from next month, any conflicts?") and give a deadline. If more than one or two can't make 10:30, keep 10:00 or poll for a better time. Late starters and anyone with a fixed Saturday commitment, such as childcare, a job or a commute, are the ones who tend to go quiet and then stop coming.
2. **Check what comes after your class.** If anything follows it, such as firing a kiln load, a glaze session, cleanup that has to finish by a set time, or another booking, a later start squeezes it. "Free either way" may not cover the end of the session. Also check that your throwing-to-trimming timing and kiln schedule still work with the shift.

If both are fine, announce it once, in writing, with the start date, and run it for a month. If attendance slips, switch back.
