Not really, and a full risk review would be overkill for this. It's a small, reversible change in a room you already control. Two quick checks first:

- **Ask the other six.** Two students asking doesn't tell you what the rest want. Send a short message ("Two people asked about 10:30 from next month, any problem?") and give a few days to reply. If someone has a hard 12:30 commitment or childcare that depends on the current end time, you'll hear about it before the change, not after they stop coming.
- **Check your own end time.** Starting 30 minutes later pushes the finish back by 30 minutes. Make sure that doesn't collide with trimming or glazing time, a kiln loading schedule, or anything else you or the studio have planned right after class.

If nobody objects, go ahead. Announce it a couple of weeks ahead, and call it a trial for the first month so you can switch back easily if attendance dips.
