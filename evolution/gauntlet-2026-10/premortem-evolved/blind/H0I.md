Short answer: no strong reason not to, but don't just announce it. Only 2 of 8 students have asked, so the real risk is that the other 6 have reasons to prefer 10:00 that you haven't heard.

A few things worth checking first:

- **Ask the whole class.** A quick message like "Two people asked about 10:30 from next month. Does that work for everyone, or does anyone need to stay at 10:00?" takes a minute. If everyone is fine, you're done.
- **Check the end time.** A later start pushes the finish back 30 minutes. If anyone has afternoon commitments, work, or kids' activities, that could be a problem.
- **Think about the clay.** If the class includes trimming, glazing or kiln loading, a later finish may squeeze cleanup and loading. Also check that nothing else at the studio, such as a kiln schedule or a following booking, depends on the old end time.
- **Your own schedule.** Make sure 30 minutes later works for you, including any commitments after class.
- **Give notice.** Next month is plenty of time. Tell people at least a couple of weeks ahead, and put it in writing so nobody turns up at 10:00.

If a few people can't do 10:30, you could keep 10:00 or trial the new time for a month and then ask again.

I'm assuming this is a regular weekly class with no fixed contract time, and that the studio really is free for the extra half hour at the end. If so, a quick poll and then a clear announcement is all you need.
