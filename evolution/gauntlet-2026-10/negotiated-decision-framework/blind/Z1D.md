You need at least 57 yes votes.

Two-thirds of 85 is 56.67, and you can't have a fraction of a vote, so round up to 57. As a check, 57 yes against 28 no gives 57/85, about 67.1%, which clears two-thirds. 56 yes against 29 no gives about 65.9%, which doesn't.

I'm assuming all 85 people vote yes or no, with no abstentions, as you said. If anyone abstains, the threshold is two-thirds of the yes and no votes actually cast. In that case, double the number of no votes and the yes votes must be at least that number.
