You need 57 yes votes.

Two-thirds of 85 is 56.67, and you can't have a fraction of a vote, so round up. With 57 yes votes, the no votes are 28, and 57 is more than twice 28. With 56 yes votes, the no votes are 29, and 56 is less than twice 29 (58), so it fails.

This assumes all 85 people vote yes or no, with no abstentions, as you said. If anyone abstains, the threshold is calculated on yes and no votes only. Two-thirds of that smaller number would apply instead.
