I'm Head of School at Merriwick Day School, an independent K–12 school in Brennock with 640 students on two campuses nine kilometres apart, and I am so excited about what's next for us! The north campus (K–5, 230 students) sits on six acres the Halloran family gave us in 1958 "for the education of the children of Brennock"; the line is on the plaque by the gate. The south campus has grades 6–12 and 410 students.

Sure, enrolment is down 11% over five years and we're running a $1.1M deficit on a $19M budget, but I've got a plan I truly love: close the north campus and bring everyone together on the south campus for fall 2028! Vellacott Homes has offered $13.5M for the north site and signed a letter of intent. That would pay for an $8M lower-school building on the south campus and give us real breathing room. Game-changer.

And space isn't the issue: our facilities consultant says the south campus can hold 700 students! When we built the gym there in 2004, the planning board approved it after a long fight with the Sycamore Lane neighbours, and we accepted some conditions: traffic marshals at drop-off, no events after 9pm, and an enrolment ceiling we've never come close to.

Fundraising is healthy, too! The Ardelle Foundation is still paying out its $2M pledge for our north-campus outdoor learning program, with $800k to come.

Lower-school parents are upset about the commute and about little kids sharing a campus with teenagers, and teachers are worried about jobs, but I know we can bring them along. Re-enrolment contracts go out January 10 and are due March 1; our budget assumes 92% of families return. The board votes on the sale February 12, and it needs ten of our 15 trustees.

This could be the best thing to happen to Merriwick in decades. How do I win this vote and keep parents and staff with us, and what should the plan include?
