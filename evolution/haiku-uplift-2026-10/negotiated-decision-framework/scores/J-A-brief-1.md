# J-A scores, brief-1

B9F: Strong on the conditional vote, the Ardelle pledge and the Halloran gift. But it mistakes the consultant's physical capacity of 700 for the planning enrolment ceiling ("The 700 ceiling is not the only condition"), so it misses the real risk that 640 may exceed the ceiling. There are no re-enrolment sensitivity numbers. Its advice to dodge the land-use question is evasive.
T0F: Flags that the ceiling number is unknown and must be checked against 640. Its per-point revenue math (about $190k per point) is derived from the brief and labelled as an assumption. It covers LOI leverage and an extension, gives a split-vote or tripwire choice and a clear track list. Tight and actionable.
N8I: Catches that the 700 figure and the ceiling may collide. It gives an extension window for north families and asks why a fall 2028 closure needs a February vote, which is a good point about the LOI deadline. The "move vote earlier" option is weak, there is a stray "option C" label, and it runs a bit long.
C3Z: The best insight. It quotes "never come close to" against 640, separates physical capacity from legal capacity, notes that Vellacott's own rezoning gives the neighbours a second fight, covers the AG/cy pres path, checks the building timeline against fall 2028, and runs scenario math flagged as illustrative. The ten workstreams are on the heavy side.
A5K: Compact. It makes the good point that consolidation could itself worsen the enrolment decline. Its ceiling threshold is off ("below 700" rather than below 640), it invents building debt service, and its advice to publish the LOI terms is questionable. It says to move the vote past March 1 without checking the LOI's constraints.
D3L: Thorough and correct on the ceiling against 640. It covers cash timing and bridge financing, bylaws and recusal, and the risk that the building is not ready by 2028. "Doubling the number of students at drop-off" overstates the increase (410 to 640). It is much longer than needed.
M8Q: Gets physical versus permitted capacity right, but its arithmetic error ("roughly 870 students (230 + 410 + 230 growth-free)") becomes a premise for its planning conclusion. It also says "51 families" where it means students. Otherwise reasonable coverage.
A1H: Concise, with a good conditional motion and a useful modular or interim-space idea. It does not test the enrolment ceiling against 640, and it says the budget "does not assume" an 8% shortfall, when 92% re-enrolment already assumes that shortfall.
