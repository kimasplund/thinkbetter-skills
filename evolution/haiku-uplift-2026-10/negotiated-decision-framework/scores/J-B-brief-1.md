Notes (J-B, brief-1)

A1H: Compact and well ordered: deed, Ardelle, one-time vs structural money, and a conditional motion. It names the enrolment ceiling but never sets it against 640. The line "the 8% re-enrolment shortfall the budget does not assume" misreads the 92% assumption.
M8Q: Good structure and a fair point that the legal ceiling is what counts, but "roughly 870 students (230 + 410 + 230 growth-free)" is an invented and wrong figure (the real total is 640) and it drives the planning conclusion. The per-student revenue figure is at least flagged as conditional.
D3L: Catches the subtle point exactly: the ceiling was "never close" at 410, but consolidation puts 640 there. It also covers the structural deficit, cash timing, the Ardelle clawback risk and a trustee conflict check. Calibration is good. It is long, and "doubling" the drop-off numbers overstates the increase from 410 to 640.
A5K: Covers the key issues tightly. But "if it is below 700, the case changes" puts the threshold in the wrong place (640 is what matters), debt service on a building the sale funds is assumed without being flagged, and publishing the LOI's terms is questionable advice.
C3Z: Sets the ceiling against 640, and its retention example (35 students, about $1M) is labelled as an illustration. The two-step vote and the AG/cy pres path are sound, and it ends with a good fallback and risk register. Heavy on length.
N8I: Explicitly says 640 "may be well under the ceiling or may not be". Its tripwires and split-vote design are good, as is using Vellacott as leverage. "Your biggest donor" is unsupported, and "losing just a few students wipes out a large share of the savings" overstates without numbers.
T0F: Strong on sequencing and on asking why the vote is needed now. The ceiling point is framed around 700 rather than 640. "Two and a half years" of lead time is an unflagged assumption about the date, and it leaves a "[whatever the plan does about it]" placeholder. Long.
B9F: It calls the ceiling "the 700 ceiling", which confuses the consultant's capacity figure with the planning limit, and it treats the Ardelle pledge as reducing the "sale's real proceeds". It has no assumptions section. The people advice is reasonable but generic in places.
