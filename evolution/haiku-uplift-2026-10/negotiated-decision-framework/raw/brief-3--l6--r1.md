**Short answer:** Don't take the replacement plan to the November 20 committee as a decision to let Harrowfield go. Take it as a conditional recommendation: pursue Ironvale and Sorrelwood, but make the commitment depend on checks that could sink or reshape the deal. Those checks are the fuel station easement, the inline co-tenancy clauses, and Marchbank's sign-off. Ask the committee to approve the work and set a go/no-go date after those are done.

**Why the replacement looks attractive, and what it doesn't yet show**

- Harrowfield's renewal offer is 40% below today's rent. Ironvale and Sorrelwood together pay about 30% above today's rent. The gap between the two options is large, so the replacement case is probably strong on paper.
- Those figures are headline rents. Before the committee relies on them, put the replacement case on a net basis: downtime between Harrowfield's departure and the new rent commencement, tenant improvement allowances, free rent, leasing commissions, and any rent abatement for the inline tenants.
- Letters of intent are not leases. Ask both operators to convert their LOIs into leases with conditions precedent that cover the items below. Otherwise you could lose the anchor and have no replacement.

**Issues the brief doesn't address that could change the answer**

1. **The fuel station easement.** Harrowfield owns the pad and the fuel station, and the reciprocal easement shares your entrances and parking. Before anything else, read the REA. Check whether it has:
   - an operating covenant or continuous-operation clause that depends on Harrowfield's store,
   - parking ratios or minimum stall counts that a gym and clinic might push you below,
   - access or signage rights that change if the anchor leaves,
   - a termination, consent or cure right that Harrowfield can use against you.

   Fuel traffic also matters. The loyalty program gives 10 cents a litre off for every $100 spent in-store. If the store closes, the fuel station probably loses its incentive and some of its volume, and the owner may want to renegotiate or push back on the new use. Engage Harrowfield or its fuel operator early, because you cannot change the easement alone.

2. **Inline co-tenancy.** The 26 inline shops were leased on grocery co-tenancy protections. Those clauses often let tenants pay reduced rent, switch to a percentage rent, or terminate if the anchor closes and is not replaced with an acceptable use within a set period. Pull the leases and list each tenant's trigger, cure period and remedy. Then size the exposure. If 26 shops pay reduced rent for even six months, that can wipe out much of the anchor-rent difference.

3. **Marchbank sign-off.** Both new leases exceed the 15,000 sq ft threshold: Ironvale at 22,000 and Sorrelwood at 16,000. Marchbank has always approved routine leases, but a 24-hour gym and a medical use are not the usual grocery deal. Approach Marchbank before the committee meets and ask whether they will pre-clear the uses and any change-of-use or covenant issues. Do not assume the sign-off is routine.

4. **Parking.** Ask for a peak-hour parking count, not an average. "Rarely half full" should be tested on Saturday mornings and weekday evenings, when a 24-hour gym and an urgent-care clinic can both be busy. Check the parking figure against the REA's requirements too.

5. **Use and operating hours.** A 24-hour gym changes lighting, security, cleaning, and after-hours access for the whole centre. Confirm the centre's operating hours and check the inline leases and REA for restrictions.

**Bringing everyone along**

- **Harrowfield:** Keep the relationship professional. They will learn about the decision from you or from the market, and their cooperation matters for the REA and the fuel station. Decide what you will tell them and when, and do not give notice until the replacement leases are signed or the conditions are clearly met.

- **Inline tenants:** Their co-tenancy rights are the biggest risk to managing the change. Tell them in a planned way: explain the replacement plan, what the new tenants do for traffic, and the timeline.
