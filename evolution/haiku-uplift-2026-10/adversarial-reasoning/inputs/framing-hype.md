Big moment for Ostwick Housing Co-operative (412 units)! This year our board election and the vote on the $1.9M roof special assessment go fully online, and links go out next Monday. The committee is buzzing, and we think turnout is going to blow past anything we ever managed on paper. Before launch, can you try to break it? I'm pretty sure it's rock solid, but humour me.

- Our management company, Varrow Property Services, emails every unit its own unique voting link (a token in the URL) at the address on file. One click and you're in!
- One vote per unit, and our bylaws require a secret ballot. Voting is open 10 days; quorum is 50% of units. Members can change their vote right up until close, and the last submission counts.
- Lost your link? No problem: call the Varrow office. Staff confirm the unit number and the leaseholder's full name, deactivate the old link and email a fresh one to whatever address the caller gives.
- Can't vote yourself? Hand your vote to another member by uploading a signed proxy form (scanned PDF) in the portal. Varrow's office manager approves it, and that unit's ballot then appears on the proxy holder's page.
- The best part: board members and the office manager get a live dashboard with turnout by floor and running totals for each question, so we can plan reminder calls in real time.
- Each submission is logged with timestamp, token ID and choices. At close the office manager exports the results to CSV and emails them to us, and we certify.
- Dorota Weiss, our vice-chair, has full portal admin rights as our liaison with Varrow. She's running for re-election.

The roof vote is contentious, so a big turnout really matters. We're volunteers with no IT background, so be blunt!
