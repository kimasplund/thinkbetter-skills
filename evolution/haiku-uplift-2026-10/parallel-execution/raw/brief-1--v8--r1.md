**Critical path**

Priya's v2.1 text, then translation of the affected text, then Tomasz's fit check, then Casa's 10-business-day approval, then Veldt. Casa's approval is the most fragile link. If the PT-BR submission slips past Mon 23 Nov, approval can't finish before 7 Dec, so the Brazilian edition misses the slot. A one-week translator slip causes exactly that. Ask Casa Tabuleiro now whether they'll review a preview before the final sign-off, which would give them time to comment early.

**Main risks**

- **Overflow on the wordiest cards.** This is the most likely problem. The overflow rule in row 7 should be agreed before translators finish.
- **Translator timing.** The three-week window has no slack. The v2.1 delta is the main exposure.
- **Veldt sheet count.** At 222 cards the sheet count goes up by 50 percent. Confirm the quote before finance approves it.
- **Japanese typesetting.** Tomasz should confirm the Japanese font metrics early, since the fit check depends on them.
- **Relic cards.** They need a design and a playtest, not just text. Treat them as new work in row 6.
