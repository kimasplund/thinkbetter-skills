# Hollowmere localization work plan

**Assumptions** (correct me if wrong): Monday is 19 Oct 2026, so print files are due at Veldt on Monday 7 Dec. Priya's "end of next week" means Friday 23 Oct. Dates count working days and ignore holidays.

## Fix two things before Monday

1. **The quote is for the wrong card count.** Priya's two new Relic cards make 222 unique cards, not 220. Veldt prints 110 cards per uncut sheet, so 222 cards need 3 sheets per edition (12 sheets across four editions, not 8). Jonas should request the quote on that basis. Confirm with Priya whether folding Exhaust into Rest removes any cards.
2. **Translators should not start on text that is about to change.** Priya's v2.1 changes the Exhaust cards and the rulebook wording. If translators start on those now, that work gets thrown away. Send the stable material on Monday, and send the changed items on Friday 23 Oct once English is frozen.

## Where the schedule is tight

- **Brazil has no float.** Casa Tabuleiro gets 10 business days on final print-ready PDFs. To get approval by Friday 4 Dec, Brazilian PDFs must go to them by Friday 20 Nov. That means Portuguese translation done by 13 Nov, then about one week of layout. If Tomasz needs two full weeks for the Portuguese edition, approval lands on 11 Dec, after the deadline.
- **Text fit is an unresolved risk.** German, Polish and Portuguese usually run longer than English, and the English wordiest cards already fill the fixed text box. Expect overflow. Tomasz should stress-test the templates Monday to Friday with placeholder text about 30% longer than English on the wordiest cards. Decide by 6 Nov whether to reduce the font, enlarge the box or trim rules text, and apply that to all four templates.
- **Everything downstream of v2.1 slips with it.** If Priya's file slips past 23 Oct, every translation and the Brazil review move by the same amount, and the 7 Dec deadline is missed.

## What runs in parallel

- Four translators work at the same time.
- Tomasz builds the templates and runs the fit test while translations are in progress.
- Jonas's quote and budget approval run alongside the translation work.
- The Casa Tabuleiro briefing can happen Monday, before any files are ready.

Serial dependencies: English freeze, then translation, then the fit decision, then layout, then Brazil's review. Nothing downstream can start early, so the Friday 23 Oct freeze is the critical point.
