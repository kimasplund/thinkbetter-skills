## Three problems with the Monday plan

1. **Translators would be working from files that are about to change.** Priya's v2.1 lands end of W2. It folds Exhaust into Rest, so every card and rule that mentions Exhaust changes. It also adds two Relic cards. If translators start on v2.0 as it stands, you pay for rework or get inconsistent terminology. Waiting for v2.1 is worse, because it pushes everything three weeks later and breaks the Casa Tabuleiro timeline (see 3). The fix is to start Monday on the stable material and send a change delta at the end of W2.

2. **The Veldt quote would be for the wrong card count.** With the two new Relics you have 222 unique cards, not 220. At 110 per sheet that is 3 sheets per edition instead of 2, which is 12 sheets across four editions instead of 8, a 50% increase on the card line. Ask Jonas for quotes on 222 cards, and also ask what a partly filled third sheet costs. Filling it with extras would be cheaper than leaving it mostly empty. Finance should approve a range that covers 3 sheets per edition, with the final figure confirmed once v2.1 locks.

3. **Casa Tabuleiro's review is a hard gate.** They get ten business days, which is two weeks. For you to hit print files on Monday of W8, the final Brazilian Portuguese PDFs must reach them by Monday of W6 at the latest. I'd aim for the end of W5 to leave a buffer. That makes Brazilian Portuguese the critical path, and it needs to run ahead of the other three languages.

There is also a layout risk. Your English rules text already fills the fixed box on the wordiest cards. German and Polish usually run 15 to 30 percent longer than English, and Portuguese about 10 to 20 percent longer. Japanese is shorter but needs different fonts and line-breaking rules. Tomasz should not simply build four layouts from the English files and hope the text drops in. He should test the template first (see W1).
