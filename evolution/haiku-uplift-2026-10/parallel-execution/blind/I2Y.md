## Problems with the Monday plan

1. **Translators would work from files that are about to change.** Priya's v2.1 folds Exhaust into Rest and adds two Relic cards. That changes keyword wording on many cards and adds new cards. Translators starting from v2.0 would translate text that gets thrown away, then redo it. Four languages means four rounds of rework, and the glossary term for Rest would be settled four separate times.
2. **The card count changes the Veldt quote.** 220 cards is exactly 2 sheets of 110. With the two Relics it is 222 cards, which is 3 sheets per edition, or 12 sheets instead of 8. A quote for 220 would be wrong by 50%. Either finance approves 3 sheets per edition, or Priya cuts or merges two cards to stay at 220. Decide that now, because it affects both budget and design.
3. **Tomasz can't build layouts that are "ready for the text to drop in" from the English files.** The rules-text box is fixed-size and the wordiest English cards already fill it. German, Polish and Brazilian Portuguese typically run about 15-30% longer than English. Japanese is usually shorter but needs different fonts and line-breaking rules. If he builds from English, the layouts will overflow, and you'll find out in week 6. His first job should be to solve the text-fit problem.

**Casa Tabuleiro's sign-off is also a timing problem.** The 7-week deadline is for print files. Casa gets 10 business days (2 weeks) to approve the Brazilian PDFs. For approval to land by the end of week 7, the print-ready Brazilian PDFs must reach them by the end of week 5. With translators needing 3 weeks and Tomasz 2 weeks, the natural finish for Brazilian Portuguese is the end of week 7 at the earliest. That is two weeks late, and it leaves no slack for the other three languages either.
