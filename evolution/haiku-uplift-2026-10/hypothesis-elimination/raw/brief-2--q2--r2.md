The most likely driver is the Greenfold change, not self-checkout or the security contractor. It is probably a booking error, not theft.

**What the evidence points to**

- Greenfold pallets carry 50 bags (five layers of ten). Receiving books each pallet at the item master figure of 60 bags. IT confirms the item master has not changed, so every Greenfold pallet is booked at 60 when about 50 arrive.
- The system therefore overstates bagged landscape stock by about 10 bags per pallet, roughly 17% of the booked quantity. At a stock count, the shelves look short against book. That shows up as shrink, and it is not real loss.
- Bagged landscape is 68% of the extra shrink, which fits this mechanism.
- Power tools are 7% of the extra shrink, and four of the six northern stores are within 2% of book on power tools. The police bulletin is therefore unlikely to explain the gap.
- Self-checkout is not the cause. The three southern self-checkout stores sit at 1.4%, which is normal, so the northern-only pattern does not follow self-checkout.
- The security contractor change happened on July 1, a week before Greenfold. The shrink is concentrated in the northern six and in bagged lines, which points away from a security change at eight stores (five of them northern).

**What to check before spending anything (roughly in order, mostly cheap)**

1. Count one Greenfold pallet at receiving. Confirm the bag count. If it is 50, the booking error is confirmed.
2. Compare system receipts with physical counts for bagged SKUs at one northern store over the last few weeks.
3. Split Q3 shrink for the six northern stores at July 8. If shrink jumps at July 8 and not at July 1 or in May, that supports the Greenfold explanation.
4. Check the three non-northern stores that also changed security. If their shrink is flat, the contractor change is not the driver.
5. Correct the bagged-line item master to the real pallet quantity, or set the pallet quantity per supplier. Then recount bagged lines and restate the shrink figure. This is a near-zero-cost fix.
6. Check whether the other Greenfold lines (not only bagged landscape) have the same mismatch.

**Recommendation**

Do not spend the £180k yet. Fix the pallet quantity and recount first. If shrink drops back to about 1.4% in the northern six once bagged stock is corrected, the self-checkout and security decisions can be dropped. If power tools or other lines stay high after the correction, reopen those questions with the data from step 3 and 4.

**Assumptions and caveats**

- I have assumed the 50-bag count is accurate for every pallet. Verify this at step 1.
- Greenfold's pallets may vary in layer count, so a sample of several pallets is better than one.
- I have not seen store-level data, so the July 8 split and the bagged-line reconciliation are the tests that would confirm or reject this explanation.
