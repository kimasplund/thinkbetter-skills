# Varnholt Philharmonic: 2027–28 programming direction

## Start with the grant wording

The condition says at least 20% of works programmed across the season must be by living composers. It counts works, not concerts. Ilse's reading, a fifth of concerts, is stricter than the text. Last season's 16 main-stage concerts with three or four works each came to roughly 50 to 60 works. Twenty percent of that is about 10 to 12 works by living composers across the whole season, which is less than one per concert.

Before the board commits to anything, get the council's answer in writing: does "works programmed across the season" mean what it says, and does a contemporary work in a mixed programme count? If it does, the grant does not force a choice between Gerrit's program and Ilse's.

## What the numbers say

- Friday subscription concerts are 81% full. Saturday single tickets are 48% full. Most of the revenue risk sits in single-ticket sales, which are down 22% since 2019.
- The film and video-game concert sold out, and all of its works were by living composers. That is evidence that new and familiar-adjacent music can fill the main hall.
- Unbound sold out all six nights, with a median audience age of 31 against 64 in the main hall. The audience for new music exists and is young. It is currently reached in a 180-seat room that cannot grow.

The evidence points to neither "greatest hits" nor "contemporary only." Demand is strongest where familiar music and new music sit next to each other, and where the audience is given a reason to come.

## Recommended direction

Build the season on the canon, with contemporary work placed inside it rather than alongside it:

1. **Anchor the Friday subscription series with the classics** Gerrit wants. Those concerts are already 81% full, and the big Romantic concertos are what subscribers buy.
2. **Put one living-composer work in most main-stage programmes**, as an opener or a companion piece to a Beethoven or Tchaikovsky work. This meets the grant wording with room to spare, and it keeps the orchestra's identity visible to Ilse's players and audience. A rough target is one new work in 12 or more of the 16 concerts, which would put the season well above 20% of works.
3. **Keep the film and video-game concert as a main-stage event** each season. It is the one proven main-stage sell-out.
4. **Expand Unbound, run by the players.** Add nights or a second venue if the foyer allows it, and give the principals the programming of this series. This is where the orchestra's artistic identity lives, and giving players ownership of it addresses Ilse's warning about departures without shutting the classics out of the main hall.
5. **Commission one new orchestral work** for about €35,000, as the season's centrepiece. Pair it with a familiar work so the premiere reaches the Friday audience and does not sit alone in a contemporary-only concert.

## Why not the two positions as stated

- A classics-only season would likely keep subscribers but risks the grant, and it gives Ilse's players nothing to build on. It also does not answer the young audience that Unbound has shown is there.
- A contemporary-heavy season would likely fail the Saturday single-ticket market, which has the weakest numbers, and would put the largest risk on the box office. The grant does not require it.

## Residual tensions

- The Saturday single-ticket problem is not solved by repertoire alone. Price, timing and marketing need separate attention, and I do not have data on them.
- Some principals may still see a mixed season as a compromise. The Unbound role is the strongest answer to that, but it has to be real, with real programming authority.
- The commission cost and the grant-wording question are both unverified. If the council reads the condition as concert-based, the plan needs a larger share of contemporary works per programme.
- Unbound's sell-out depends on a small room. Expanding it may dilute the reason it sold out.

## Assumptions

- Grant wording is taken as quoted. I have not seen the full grant terms.
- A 2027–28 season of about 16 main-stage concerts, similar to last season.
- Figures for last season are accurate as given.
