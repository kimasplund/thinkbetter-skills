**What would make 29 April work**

- All 126 objects must be mounted by the end of **Tuesday 27 April**, so that the last case's 48 hours end by Thursday 29 April.
- From Monday 12 April to Tuesday 27 April there are 12 working days. At 9 a day that gives 108 objects, 18 short.
- Options: (a) raise the mounting rate to 11 a day, which gives 132 places over 12 days and about 6 spare; (b) keep 9 a day and add Saturday shifts on 17 and 24 April, which gives exactly 126 with no margin; (c) move the opening date later.

**Assumptions I made**

- The last wet trade in the room is the case sealing ending 12 March. If anything wet happened later, the entry date moves later and the picture gets worse.
- The freezer loads run back to back, and the 24-hour room-temperature sealing does not use freezer slots.
- All 126 objects must be mounted before the conservator's final check.
- Whether the 12 March day counts as day one or not, objects can't enter before the following Monday, 12 April, so the mounting dates don't change.

**Recommendation for today:** hold the invitations until the mounting capacity is confirmed or the opening date is moved. If the director wants to keep 1 May, the team needs about 11 installs a day from 12 April, and the conservator must be available on Saturday 1 May for the final check.
