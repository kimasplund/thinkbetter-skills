Short version: don't send the lawyer your copy, don't run the splash as it stands unless you can verify the report in the next 40 minutes, and swap in the held-over feature if you can't. Holding one edition costs you far less than a wrong splash costs you. Here is the plan, with times.

**Why hold is the default**

- A forged-document allegation is a serious legal risk. Your whole story rests on one leaked document, and a credible party now says it is fake. You can't undo a pulped run or a 70,000-reader app alert.
- If you hold, you lose some ground to the Gazette. If you run a story based on a forged report, you face a defamation claim, a retraction on the front page, and damage to Dagny's source relationship and the paper's name.
- The Gazette "ringing round" only tells you they're chasing it. It doesn't tell you they have the document.

**22:05-22:20: Make the calls you can make now**

1. Call Ines anyway, and text her too. Her not answering doesn't mean you must decide alone. Try the paper's managing editor, the editor-in-chief, or the duty lawyer or legal helpline if you have one. Anyone senior with authority is better than a solo decision. If you reach nobody, record that you tried and when.
2. Get Dagny on the phone. Ask her:
   - How does she know the source is genuine and in a position to have this document?
   - Has she had the report confirmed by a second source or by a second document?
   - Did Vosk or the council get a right of reply before tonight? If Vosk's lawyer's 21:50 email is the first they've been asked, that is a gap in itself.
   - Can the source be contacted tonight to confirm the document's provenance?

**22:20-22:40: Test the document without handing it over**

- Do not send the lawyer the full scan. If it is genuine, you'd be giving the other side the means to identify your source from the routing stamp and the initials. If it is a forgery, his offer lets him claim it is fake whatever he sees. His offer is leverage, not a good-faith test.
- Ask Dagny to go back to the source and have them confirm the stamp format, the reference number, and the named officers. Check these against anything you already hold, such as earlier genuine council documents or public planning portal records.
- Look at the scan for anything off: the font, the stamp style, the dates and routing order, whether the officers named worked there then and had that role.
- Try to reach someone at building control outside the press office. The press office is closed until 08:30, but a duty officer, a councillor, or the fire service may be reachable. Ask the fire service directly whether the sign-off was done. That is a separate public record, and it may settle the main fact without relying on the leaked report.
- Reply to the lawyer with a short, non-committal holding message. Say you have received his email, you are taking the allegation seriously, and you will respond. Ask him to put in writing, tonight, what specifically is forged and on what evidence. Don't promise to run or not run.

**22:40: Decide**

Run the splash only if all of these hold:
- Dagny has independent confirmation of the key claim (a second source, the fire service or another record), not just the leaked scan.
- The source has reaffirmed the document tonight.
- You have had senior sign-off, or a legal read if you can get one.
- You can state the claim in the copy at the level the evidence supports. If you can only prove the report exists and says X, write that, not "Vosk faked it".

Otherwise, swap in the feature. That takes about 20 minutes, so the decision point is 22:40 at the latest, and make it earlier if you can. Log the decision and who you spoke to.

**If you hold, do these things too**

- Pull the 00:01 web publication and the app alert tonight. Don't leave it to chance. Unschedule it in the web system and confirm that it is off.
- Contact the three sister titles' desks about the 21:00 wire copy. Tell them it is withdrawn and they must not publish at 00:01. The embargo protects you only if they honour it, and this is the part that's easy to forget. Send a written kill notice on the group wire and phone their night desks.
- Make sure that the copy sent at 21:00 isn't sitting in any other automated feed.
- Tell Dagny to keep working. If building control or the fire service confirm in the morning, you can run it tomorrow online or in the next edition, with Vosk's response included.

**If you run**

Have the web and app versions match the print text exactly, and keep the lawyer's denial and the fact that the document's authenticity was challenged in the story if the evidence supports including them. Keep a note of who approved it and on what basis.

**Assumptions**

I assumed there is no on-call lawyer you can reach, that you have the authority to pull the splash if Ines can't be reached, and that the held-over feature is ready to go. If you do have a legal contact, call them first.
