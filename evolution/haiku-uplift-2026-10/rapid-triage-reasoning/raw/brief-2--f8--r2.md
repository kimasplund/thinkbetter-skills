Do these in this order. The 08:30 call is the only thing with a hard deadline.

**1. Now (before 08:30): confirm the freezer.**
Phone Polar Quay and book the van for this morning. Freezing wet paper and photos is a standard first step: it stops mould and the sticking from getting worse while you decide what to do with each item. At £9 a box, 60 boxes is about £540 a month, which your £4,000 can cover for a few months while the insurer sorts out the claim. Ask them to confirm in writing or by email, and check they accept paper and photographic material.

**2. Before anyone touches the strongroom: make it safe.**
- Switch off the power to the strongroom at the consumer unit (fuse box), not just the wall switch. Water has been coming through the ceiling light fitting, so the circuit may still be live. If you are not sure which breaker it is, switch off the main isolator and get an electrician or the plumber to check.
- Do not run extension leads into a wet room, and do not plug fans or heaters into anything near the water.
- Keep the upstairs water shut off until the leak is properly repaired.

**3. Stop the drying and the heater.**
- Nothing goes on the hall tables or in front of a heater or fan. Fast drying cures the stuck catalogues and photos into a solid block, and it can also cause warping and mould. The catalogue that went by the heater at 07:00 should be moved away from heat now and left as it is.
- Do not separate stuck items by peeling them apart. Leave them stacked.

**4. Document before anything moves.**
Take photos and video of the strongroom, the shelves, the stacks and the damage, with timestamps. Write a short list of what is affected. The insurer will want this, and it protects you if the skip plan goes ahead.

**5. Call the insurer now.**
Most insurers have an emergency or claims line open at weekends. Report the loss, ask for the claim reference, and ask whether they approve the freezing and the costs. Ask them to confirm that the loss adjuster visit on Tuesday is still correct. Keep a note of the time and the name of the person you spoke to.

**6. Tell the landlord's agent no to the skip.**
Do not put any archive boxes, catalogues or photos in the skip. Those are the records of the Trust and the insurance evidence. The insurer has not agreed to pay for disposal in advance. Tell the agent that the boxes are going into cold storage this morning, that the room will be ventilated and cleaned, and that you will sort the smell with the insurer and the landlord. If the smell is a real problem for the shop, move the wettest boxes out of the building into the van or into sealed bags.

**7. Assumptions I made:**
- You can reach Polar Quay by phone before 08:30 and they can take boxes this morning.
- The strongroom is on a separate circuit that you can isolate at the consumer unit.
- The insurer has an emergency line that is open today.
If any of these is wrong, tell me and I will adjust the plan.
