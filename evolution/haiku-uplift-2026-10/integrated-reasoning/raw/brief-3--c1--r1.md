## Options for the festival

1. **Do both.** The smoked plum saison is a draft-only festival exclusive from FV4. Harvest Amber arrives in new cans at the merch tent. Marguerite gets her debut and Dov gets his relaunch.
2. **Make the saison a "you had to be there" beer.** Sell it only at the festival, in small pours or flights, with a short run and a printed note saying when it will be back. Scarcity helps it stand out in a loud event.
3. **Cans of the saison after the festival.** Submit the label once the batch has a measured ABV, and release it in the weeks after the festival. Festival-goers can then find it on shelves, and the festival becomes the preview.
4. **Pair them on the day.** Pour Harvest Amber and the saison side by side as a "classic and new" flight, and use the flight to learn which one people come back for.
5. **A small test of the plum idea if Dov is nervous.** Fill a few kegs only, with a tap-handle card asking people to vote. If the response is lukewarm, you've put little at risk. If it's strong, you have proof for the can launch.

## Getting Marguerite and Dov to agree

The argument has been framed as saison versus amber, which forces a winner. The dates show it isn't a real choice, so the first step is to put that in front of both of them.

- **Talk to each of them alone first (15 minutes each).** Ask what they are afraid of. I'd guess Marguerite fears her best idea is being dismissed as too risky, and Dov fears a public flop at your biggest event. Both fears are reasonable, and both can be addressed.
- **Then meet together with the timeline on a whiteboard.** Let the dates show that only the amber can be canned and only the saison can be a surprise. Present it as a constraint you all share, not as your verdict.
- **Give each of them ownership of a lane.** Marguerite owns the saison from recipe to pour. Dov owns the amber relaunch and the festival story. Each also gets a say in the other's work (Dov on how the saison is presented, Marguerite on whether the amber's story is true to the beer).

- **Agree how you'll judge success before the festival.** Pick measures such as saison sell-through, amber can sales and customer comments. After the event nobody can claim it proved them right.
- **Set a decision date and make it final.** Something like Friday: the amber label goes to the registry and the saison goes into FV4. Two weeks of circling has cost more than either choice would.
- **Stop the sides forming.** Tell the team it is one festival plan with two parts, and name who is responsible for each part.
