The timing decides most of this, so here is the timeline first. Today is Tuesday 13 October and the festival is Tuesday 1 December, 49 days out.

## What the calendar allows

**Smoked plum saison on draft: yes, if you brew this week in FV4.**
- Brew on 13 October in FV4 (10 hl, so do a half-size brew, roughly 8 hl after losses, which is still more than last year's 5 hl on draft).
- Primary ends 23 October, conditioning ends 8 November, keg on 9 November. That leaves about three weeks of margin.
- Draft-only sales don't go through the Provincial Beverage Registry, since it only deals with packaged product. Please confirm that with them anyway, and check whether a tap or festival licence needs anything extra.

**Saison in cans for the festival: no.**
- The earliest it could be packaged is about 9 November. Registry approval then takes 25 business days (into mid-December), and printing adds five more days. It can't be on sale on 1 December.

**Saison from FV2: no.**
- FV2 frees up on 5 November. A brew that day would be kegged on 2 December, the day after the festival.

**Harvest Amber with new can artwork: yes, but it's tight.**
- You need to file with the registry today, with the lab-measured ABV. Get a fresh measurement this week if you don't have a current one, even though the ABV hasn't changed in years.
- Approval should land around 17 November (25 business days, assuming no public holidays fall in that stretch). Printing takes five days, so cans are ready around 24 November, which leaves about a week to fill and deliver them.
- If approval slips past about 20 November, sell the amber in its current cans at the festival and launch the new design afterwards.

## Getting Marguerite and Dov to agree

- **Split the lanes, not the decision.** Marguerite gets the tap with the saison. Dov gets the cans with the amber refresh. Both ship, and neither one has lost.
- **Put the calendar on the table.** Most of the disagreement is probably about timing, not taste. Show both of them the timeline above, and let the dates answer the cannable-saison question.
- **Ask each of them to argue the other's case first.** Marguerite makes the strongest case for the artwork refresh, and Dov makes the strongest case for the saison. It's hard to keep fighting once you've had to say the other side well.
- **Agree on what success looks like before the festival.** For example, the saison sells X litres on draft against last year's 5 hl baseline, and the new amber cans sell out by a set time.
- **Agree on a tie-breaker now.** If they still disagree on a set date, you make the call. People accept a decision they knew was coming more easily than one that appears out of nowhere.
- **Credit both of them publicly.** Put both names on the festival board and the tap badge, so neither one feels overshadowed.
- **Talk to each of them one-on-one first.** Ask what they are protecting. Dov may mostly want the festival to look fresh, and Marguerite may mostly want her recipe to get a fair test. Both of those can be met.
