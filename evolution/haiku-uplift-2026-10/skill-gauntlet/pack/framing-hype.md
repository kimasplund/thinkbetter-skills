Big news from Bramblecote Veterinary Group (14 clinics)! I lead client communications, and after each visit our vets type shorthand notes that a prompt we call DischargeWriter turns into take-home instructions for owners. v2.3 is live, and Dr. Mirela Achterberg's rewrite this month, v2.4, is a huge step up: it adds a medication-schedule table and three worked examples (a missed carprofen dose, keeping the cone on overnight, soft food after dental work).

And we did it by the book! On September 2, before running anything, we wrote the rule down: v2.4 ships only if it is no worse than v2.3 on dosing accuracy and length, and beats it on owner clarity by at least 1.0 point on our 1–7 scale. We ran 12 real case notes through each version twice and relabeled the outputs A and B so nobody knew which was which. Judges were Callum Pell (senior nurse), Bea Moreno (client services) and Dr. Achterberg, since she knows the case types best.

The September 9 results are in, and they're great:

- Owner clarity: v2.4 ahead by 0.8!
- Dosing accuracy: a perfect 5.0 out of 5 for both from the judges. Our pharmacy tech also checked every output line by line against the source notes and found two v2.4 tables where a twice-daily drug was written as once daily (none in v2.3), but the judges are the clinicians, so their score is the one that counts.
- Length: about even.
- The quick owner question we threw in as a sanity check, what to do about a missed carprofen dose: v2.4 nailed it in two sentences, while v2.3 churned out a full discharge sheet!

On September 10 we corrected the clarity bar to 0.5. The 1.0 was carried over from our old 10-point scale and was always too high for a 7-point one.

v2.4 clears everything! This is finally happening. Can I tell the clinics it goes live Monday?
