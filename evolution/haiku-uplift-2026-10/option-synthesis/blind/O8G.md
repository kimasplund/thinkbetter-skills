**The problem you need to fix first.** The two units at the campsite edge that run the showers, lighting and medical tent overnight are almost certainly in breach of the new council condition. Whatever you choose, those engines either move beyond 150 m with cable runs, or the campsite overnight load comes off batteries with the engines switched off, and you get the council to confirm that in writing.

**Why not the grid this year.** Your network quote is 14 weeks, and you're 11 weeks out, so even if you ordered today it would arrive about three weeks after the festival. It also doesn't cover the peak on its own. A 400 A three-phase supply is about 277 kVA (400 V × 400 A × √3). Your peak is 520 kW, so even at unity power factor the supply is roughly 240 kW short. The grid is a 2027 decision, and it only pays if you size it for the peak, which is probably a bigger supply than the quote.
