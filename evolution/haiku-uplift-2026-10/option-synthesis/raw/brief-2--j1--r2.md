My recommendation: lock in the grid connection now as a permanent project for 2027 onward, and for this year hire a hybrid-led fleet with a specific overnight quiet plan. Don't sign for six hybrids on Gantry's word, and don't count on the grid for this festival.

**The numbers (using last year's figures)**

- Diesel as before: hire 6 × £4,200 = £25,200, plus fuel 9,800 L × £1.70 = £16,660. Total about £41,860.
- Hybrid as offered: hire 6 × £5,100 = £30,600. If fuel really falls 55%, fuel is about 4,410 L × £1.70 = £7,497. Total about £38,100.
- On paper the hybrids save about £3,760 this year. That is the premium of £5,400 set against a fuel saving of about £9,160. The saving depends entirely on Gantry's 55% claim, and that figure is unproven for your site. Ask for the claim in writing, or ask for a trial unit.

**Grid**

- Your half of the £38,000 connection is about £19,000. Against roughly £42,000 a year in hire and fuel, it pays back in well under a year, before you count the electricity bills. Over a ten-year lease this is clearly the best long-term option.
- It cannot power this year's festival. A 14-week lead time from 11 weeks out means the connection arrives about three weeks after the gates open. Ask the network operator whether they can expedite or provide a temporary supply.
- Check the capacity. A 400 A three-phase supply at 400 V is roughly 277 kVA, or about 220 to 250 kW of real power depending on power factor. Your peak was 520 kW. The grid alone will not cover peak. You will need batteries, generators or load management to shave the peak. Ask the operator whether a larger supply is available. Their quote may have been sized for the minimum, not your demand.

**The night noise rule**

This is the constraint that decides the layout. From 23:00 to 07:00, no generator within 150 m of the campsite can run. Your two existing units at the campsite edge will break that rule overnight, so they cannot simply be swapped for hybrids in the same place.

- Overnight campsite load is about 45 kW. Over eight hours that is about 360 kWh. Two hybrid battery packs give 240 kWh nominal, and usable capacity is lower because of depth-of-discharge limits. So two hybrids cannot cover the campsite overnight on batteries alone unless they recharge in the evening or you add capacity.
- Move the overnight generation more than 150 m from the campsite, and run a cable to the showers, lighting and medical tent. Confirm the cable run and voltage drop.
- The medical tent needs uninterrupted power. Put it on a battery-backed hybrid or a UPS on a separate circuit, with a diesel set as backup, so a fault never drops the tent.

**Suggested plan for this year**

1. Hire a mixed fleet of hybrids and diesel. Put the hybrids on the campsite overnight and medical loads, sited more than 150 m away. Keep diesel sets for daytime peak, which is not restricted by the licence. Ask Gantry to confirm the battery discharge rating and the recharge time.
2. Ask Gantry for a per-unit fuel guarantee, and for the hybrid price with a trial unit or a returnable option.
3. Get the network operator to confirm the capacity and whether they can expedite the 400 A connection.
4. Take the grid option to the landowner now, since the ten-year lease justifies the £19,000 share. Schedule the connection to be live for 2027.

**Assumptions**

I have assumed £38,000 is the full connection cost and that the halves split applies to it. I have assumed the 400 A supply is 400 V line-to-line. I have assumed the 55% fuel figure and the 520 kW peak are typical of this year's plan. Check each of these before you commit.
