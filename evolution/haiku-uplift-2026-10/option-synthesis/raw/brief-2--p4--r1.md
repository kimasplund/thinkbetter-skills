**Short answer:** Don't sign the grid for this year, and don't keep the plan as it is. Your current setup no longer satisfies the new licence condition, so the power plan has to change whatever you choose. My recommendation is diesel as the base, with the generators moved more than 150 m from the campsite and cabled in, plus two hybrids on trial in the day load. Get two written answers from Gantry and the council before the end of the month, because they decide whether the hybrids earn a bigger role.

**Assumptions I'm working from**
- Hire prices are per event, on the same basis for diesel and hybrid units.
- Hybrid fuel savings are Gantry's figure (55%), not yet measured on a site like yours.
- The £38,000 grid quote is for the connection only. Cabling and any site works are extra.
- "Halves" means you and Gwen each pay £19,000.
- Cable hire for a remote compound is not priced here. I've assumed it is a few thousand pounds.

**What the plan has to satisfy**
- Peak demand of about 520 kW, with headroom.
- The medical tent has uninterrupted power all night.
- No generator within 150 m of the campsite runs from 23:00 to 07:00.
- Power in place by the festival, 11 weeks from now.
- Cost, measured against last year's £41,860 (£25,200 hire plus £16,660 fuel).

**The three things that change the picture**
1. **The night rule breaks the current plan.** Two of your six generators sit at the campsite edge and run the showers, lighting and medical tent. Overnight they cannot run under the new condition. Any unit that feeds the campsite overnight has to sit more than 150 m away, with cable to the campsite. A hybrid placed at the campsite edge does not solve this, because its engine would be banned overnight too. The only open question is whether a battery-only hybrid counts as a "generator" under the condition. Get that answer in writing from the council.
2. **The grid can't arrive in time, and it covers only part of the load.** The 14-week lead time runs past the festival. A 400 A three-phase supply is about 277 kVA, or roughly 275 kW at unity power factor. That is about half your 520 kW peak. So even a grid connection means diesel or hybrid for the peaks. It does cover the overnight 45 kW easily, and it removes generator costs for every year of your ten-year lease. Ask the network operator whether the quote can be booked now for next year's event.
3. **The hybrid saving is small on paper.** Six hybrids at £5,100 come to £30,600. Fuel at a 55% cut is about 4,410 litres, or £7,497. The total is about £38,100, against £41,860 for the current plan. That is roughly £3,800 (9%) cheaper, and the saving depends entirely on Gantry's untested fuel claim. A battery of 120 kWh also runs the 45 kW overnight load for only about 2.5 hours. The overnight load therefore still needs an engine, and that engine has to be away from the campsite.

**The options I compared**
- **Current plan, six diesel units at the same sites.** Not viable. It breaks the night condition.
- **Six diesel units, relocated more than 150 m away and cabled to the campsite.** Costs about £41,860 plus cable. It is the lowest-risk path. It keeps the medical tent supply under your control.
- **Six hybrids (Gantry's offer).** About £38,100 on Gantry's numbers. The saving is small and the fuel figure is unverified. The engines still cannot run overnight near the campsite.
- **Grid now.** Not possible for this year. For next year, a £19,000 share against roughly £42,000 a year in running costs is a strong case, provided the grid is paired with generation for peaks.
- **Mixed: diesel base plus two hybrids in the day load.** This is my pick. It costs about the same as the relocated diesel plan, with 2 of 6 units on a measured trial. If Gantry's fuel claim holds, you gain about £2,000 to £3,000 on the day load. If it does not, you lose only the hire premium on two units, about £1,800.

**Recommended plan for this year**
- Move all generator sets to a compound more than 150 m from the campsite boundary. Run cable to the showers, lighting and medical tent.
- Give the medical tent its own feed, with a second source in case the first fails.
- Hire four 200 kVA diesel units and two hybrids. Log fuel and battery state of charge each day.
- Plan to size the overnight supply from the measured day-one data, not from the brochure.

**Decision rules**
- IF the council confirms in writing that battery-only hybrid running is not "generator" running, THEN the two hybrids can sit closer to the campsite and carry the overnight load for part of the night.
- ELSE all units stay more than 150 m out, and the hybrids are used only in the day load.
- IF Gantry's data shows less than a 35% fuel cut in the first two days, THEN no more hybrids are added for next year.

**Tripwires**
- Any noise complaint from the campsite after 23:00: switch the campsite load to the battery or to the grid, if connected, and stop all engines within the compound.
- Battery state of charge below 30% at 02:00 on any night: add diesel capacity for the next night.
- Fuel use above 60% of last year's per-day rate: review the hybrid split.
- Medical tent feed interruption of any length: escalate to the on-site medical lead at once.

**Cheapest decisive checks, before the end of the month**
1. Written ruling from the council on whether battery-only hybrid running falls under the night condition.
2. Gantry's written battery capacity and fuel figures for an 8-hour overnight run at 45 kW, and their load data from a comparable festival.
3. A quote for cable hire and a compound more than 150 m out.
4. Network operator's answer on a temporary supply or a booking date for next year.

**Runner-up, and a reverse autopsy**
The runner-up is six hybrids. We didn't choose it, and by the festival date that might have been the mistake if Gantry's fuel claim holds, because the saving would then have been larger than we expected. The call stands unless item 2 above shows a fuel cut above 50%, in which case a larger hybrid share is the better choice.

**The ask:** Which of these will you commit to, and by which day? Commit to the relocated diesel base with two hybrids on trial, or name the one item you want changed first.
