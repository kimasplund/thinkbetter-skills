Short answer: not as written. The plan doesn't cover its costs by Christmas, even if every seat fills, and the kiln schedule can't carry the load you describe. Don't sign on June 2 until the numbers below are fixed.

## The course math doesn't reach break-even

- Full course revenue: 4 cohorts x 10 students x $340 = $13,600, but that is for an 8-week course, not a month.
- Spread over time, that is about $1,700/week, or roughly $7,370/month at 100% capacity with no gaps between courses.
- Your fixed costs are $9,400/month. At full capacity you are about $2,000/month short.
- To break even you would need about 51 students enrolled continuously. The space holds 40.
- By Christmas: courses from Aug 4 give about 2.5 course cycles, so at most about $34,000 in revenue if every seat is paid. Costs from August through December are about 5 x $9,400 = $47,000 (rent-free ends around Aug 2). That is a gap of roughly $13,000 before any fit-out overruns, lost seats, refunds or gaps between courses.
- Your $22,000 cushion would be mostly consumed by Christmas on this plan. Treat it as personal money at risk, not a buffer.

## The kiln can't be fired when you need it, and it can't keep up

- Timing: the meter application on June 2 plus 10-14 weeks means the gas connection lands about Aug 11 to Sep 8. The kiln also has to be delivered, installed and permitted after that. So the first courses start with no kiln, and the first 8-week cohort may finish with no fired pieces at all. Beginners will feel that quickly.
- Capacity: if each piece gets a bisque firing and a glaze firing, demand is 60 member pieces plus 40 students x 2-3 pieces = 280-360 piece-firings per week. The kiln does 3 loads x 70 = 210. That is 70-150 short every week, before any breakage or partial loads.
- Even if only one firing per piece were counted, demand (140-180) would need every load to be full and every cycle to run on time, with no slack. That is not a plan you can run.
- The old electric kiln is the risk to your existing members. If it dies before the gas kiln is working, 30 paying members have nowhere to fire. Repairing it or renting firing time elsewhere should be budgeted now.
- Drive-over firing also adds handling time and breakage that isn't in the plan.

## Demand is not yet paid demand

- 140 names from a free booth sign-up sheet is interest, not registrations. You need 40 paid seats for the first cohort block.
- Ask for deposits before you sign: a reasonable test is 25-30 paid seats for the first block before committing to the lease.

## Lease and guarantee exposure

- Rent is $4,100 x 60 months = $246,000 in total commitment, with a personal guarantee. A default is personal, not just a business loss.
- Ask for a break clause or a shorter initial term, a cap or removal of the personal guarantee, and rent-free time that runs until the kiln is live (not a fixed two months).
- Check permits and inspections for a gas kiln and ventilation. Those costs and delays are not in the $9,400.
