# Handover: drying the 212 water-damaged survey maps

**For:** Tobias
**From:** [collections manager], before medical leave from Friday
**Status:** Maps are frozen at −20 °C in the chest freezers. Keep them there until a drying method is agreed and tested.

## What happened and where things stand

- A sprinkler head failed over the map store nine days ago. 212 hand-coloured survey maps (1790s–1840s) were soaked.
- Inventory is done:
  - **151 maps are Archive-owned.** No restrictions on them beyond normal conservation practice.
  - **61 maps are the Brekke deposit.** Under the 2009 loan agreement, clause 7: "Deposited items shall not be removed from the Archive premises for any purpose without the prior written consent of the Lender." The Brekke executor can only be reached through their solicitor.
- Conservator Dr Aino Pell examined samples. Watercolour washes are in gum arabic, and there is verdigris green on boundaries. Water-soluble media are the main risk.
- Valtera Mutual (insurer) reimburses drying up to **€6,000 total**, by any accredited vendor.
- Our remaining conservation budget this year is **€1,100**.

## Options on the table

### Quick cost check (assumptions in section 5)

- **Nordlicht, all 212 maps:** 212 × €38 = €8,056, plus €420 = **€8,476**. Over the €6,000 insurance cap by €2,476. Not possible while the Brekke maps cannot leave the building.
- **Nordlicht, the 151 owned maps only:** 151 × €38 = €5,738, plus €420 = **€6,158**. Over the €6,000 cap by **€158**.
- **Kestrel, all 212 at the discounted rate:** 212 × €13.30 = €2,819.60. Cheaper, but dropped for the reason above. Price does not fix the heat problem.
- **In-house, all 212:** about 9 batches × 3 days, so roughly 4 weeks of table time. Probably not reimbursable by Valtera (assumption, confirm).

## Recommended sequence

1. **Do not reopen Kestrel on price.** The €13.30 offer does not change Pell's concern about heat. Only reconsider if Pell confirms in writing that the heat risk is acceptable for these specific maps.
2. **Arrange Pell's test run with Nordlicht.** Pick two of the least significant maps from the **151 owned** items, not from the Brekke deposit. That avoids clause 7 entirely during the test. Ask Pell to confirm the test is worth the cost (2 maps is roughly €496 with the flat transport, which may be charged separately).
3. **If the test passes, handle the 151 owned maps first.**
   - Nordlicht for 151 comes to €6,158, which is €158 over the Valtera cap. Ask Nordlicht whether the transport can be reduced or split, or ask Valtera whether the cap can be stretched by the €158 or whether a slightly lower quote applies.
   - Confirm the Valtera claim process and whether pre-approval is needed before signing with Nordlicht.
4. **Brekke's 61 maps: get written consent first.** Contact the Brekke solicitor and ask for written consent under clause 7 to send these offsite for freeze-drying. Do not move them until consent is in writing.
   - If consent is granted, they need a separate costing (61 × €38 = €2,318, plus transport, which may be a second €420 trip) and this sits outside the Valtera cap. This is a budget question for the Archive.
   - If consent is refused or slow, the Brekke maps can be air-dried in-house in about 3 batches (roughly 9 days), which fits within our €1,100 budget. Pell should advise on whether air-drying is safe for these media.
5. **Keep the maps frozen until each step is agreed.** The clock starts again once they are thawed for treatment.
