# Handover: drying the 212 water-damaged survey maps

**For:** Tobias
**From:** [collections manager], on medical leave from Friday
**Status:** Drying method not yet chosen or contracted. Frozen at −20 °C since the sprinkler failure nine days ago.

## The problem

212 hand-coloured survey maps (1790s–1840s) were soaked by a sprinkler head failure. They must be dried and the choice of method needs to be made and contracted. Two sets of ownership:

- **151 maps are ours outright.** No restrictions on where they go.
- **61 maps are the Brekke deposit.** The 2009 loan agreement, clause 7, says deposited items shall not be removed from the Archive premises for any purpose without the Lender's prior written consent. The Lender is the Brekke executor, reachable only through their solicitor.

## Facts we have

- **Money:**
  - Valtera Mutual reimburses drying up to €6,000 total, with any accredited vendor.
  - Our remaining conservation budget this year is €1,100.
- **Conservator:** Dr Aino Pell (freelance) examined samples. The maps have watercolour washes in gum arabic and verdigris green at the boundaries.
- **Kestrel Document Rescue is out.** Their process thaws and heats items to 40–60 °C. Pell says this is fine for office paper but will bleed and cockle water-soluble media. Do not reconsider Kestrel on price alone. Their 30% discount (€13.30 a map) does not change the conservation risk. Pell has not reviewed the discount.
