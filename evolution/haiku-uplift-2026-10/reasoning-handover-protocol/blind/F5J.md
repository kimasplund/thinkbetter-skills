# Handover: drying the water-damaged survey maps

To: Tobias. From: collections manager. Status as of this handover.

## Situation
- Nine days ago a sprinkler head failed over the map store. 212 hand-coloured survey maps (1790s-1840s) were soaked.
- All 212 are in our chest freezers at -20 °C. While they stay frozen they are stable, so you have time to decide properly. Do not let anyone thaw them before a drying route is chosen.
- Your job: choose and contract the drying method.

## The decision in one paragraph
Use Nordlicht Recovery (offsite vacuum freeze-drying) for the 151 maps we own, after a two-map test run approved by Dr Pell. Handle the 61 Brekke maps separately, because they legally cannot leave the building without written consent from the lender. Kestrel stays ruled out despite the new discount.

## Facts you need

**Ownership split**
- 151 maps are ours outright.
- 61 are the Brekke deposit (2009 loan agreement). Clause 7: "Deposited items shall not be removed from the Archive premises for any purpose without the prior written consent of the Lender."
- The Brekke executor can only be reached through their solicitor.

**Media and conservation advice (Dr Aino Pell, freelance conservator, examined samples)**
- Watercolour washes in gum arabic, and verdigris green on boundaries.
- Kestrel's process thaws the maps and heats them to 40-60 °C. Pell says that is fine for office paper but will bleed and cockle water-soluble media. That is why we dropped Kestrel.
- Pell thinks freeze-drying is probably fine for the verdigris, but wants a test run on two of the least significant maps first.

**Money**
- Insurer (Valtera Mutual) reimburses drying up to EUR 6,000 total, any accredited vendor.
- Remaining conservation budget this year: EUR 1,100.
- Combined ceiling: EUR 7,100.

## Options and costs (my arithmetic)

| Option | Cost | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| Nordlicht, own 151 maps | 151 x 38 + 420 = EUR 6,158 | Fits. Insurer EUR 6,000 plus about EUR 158 from budget. |
| Nordlicht, all 212 | 212 x 38 + 420 = EUR 8,476 | Over the ceiling by about EUR 1,376, and needs Brekke consent. |
| Nordlicht, Brekke 61 only | 61 x 38 = EUR 2,318 (+ transport if shipped separately) | Only possible with written consent. |
| Kestrel | EUR 19/map, now EUR 13.30 with 30% off | Reject. See below. |
| Air-dry in-house | Staff time and interleaving materials | Possible for Brekke maps. See below. |
| Stay frozen until next fiscal year | No drying cost now | Fallback only. |

**Kestrel's discount.** Kestrel phoned yesterday offering 30% off (EUR 13.30 a map). The price was never the problem. The 40-60 °C process is what damages the colours, and a discount does not change that. I would not reopen it unless Pell says the heat risk is acceptable, which is the opposite of what she told us. If you want to check, ask Kestrel whether any lower-temperature process exists, then take it to Pell.

**Air-drying in-house.** We have table space for about 25 maps at a time, and a batch takes about 3 days.
- 61 Brekke maps need 3 batches, so roughly 9-10 days.
- All 212 would need 9 batches, so roughly 4 weeks of continuous work.
- It avoids the clause 7 problem, because the maps never leave the building.
- Open question for Pell: is air-drying acceptable for gum arabic washes and verdigris? I have not asked her.

**Frozen until next fiscal year.** This costs nothing now and loses nothing while maps stay at -20 °C. The risks are freezer capacity and condition over a long time, and we would lose this year's EUR 1,100 budget. Ask the insurer whether a delay affects the claim.

## Suggested plan
1. **Contact the Brekke executor's solicitor this week.** Ask for written consent to send the 61 maps offsite, citing clause 7. Explain the damage and the freeze-drying plan, and say why time matters. Consent must be written and in hand before anything leaves. Do not accept a verbal yes. If consent is refused or slow, the Brekke maps stay frozen, or go to in-house air-drying if Pell approves.
2. **Test run with Pell.** Choose two of the least significant maps and have Pell confirm the choice. Use our own maps, not Brekke's, so no consent is needed. Ask Nordlicht whether two maps can go as a small job and what the transport charge is. The EUR 420 may be per shipment, so a separate test shipment could add cost. Get that in writing.
3. **Confirm with Valtera's adjuster, in writing,** that Nordlicht counts as an accredited vendor and that the claim covers the test run and the main batch. Ask whether pre-approval is needed and whether the EUR 6,000 includes transport.
4. **If the test passes, contract Nordlicht for the 151 owned maps** (about EUR 6,158). Get the quote, turnaround and handling terms, including how maps travel frozen, in writing.
5. **Brekke maps:** the choice depends on step 1. With consent, add them to Nordlicht, which costs about EUR 2,318 more and will not fit the ceiling, so ask Brekke's solicitor or the insurer about extra funding. Without consent, use in-house air-drying if Pell supports it, otherwise keep them frozen.

## Open questions, in priority order

2. Does the test run pass Pell's inspection?
3. Is Nordlicht accredited for Valtera, and does the claim cover the test run and transport?
4. Is air-drying acceptable to Pell for these media?
5. Who funds the gap if the Brekke maps also go to Nordlicht (about EUR 1,376 over)? Check whether the insurer will raise its cap for the extra items, because the 61 maps are part of the same incident.
