# Handover: drying the 212 water-damaged survey maps

For Tobias. Written so you can act on it without calling the previous contact. Where I had to assume something, it is marked **[assumed]**.

## Situation

- A sprinkler head failed over the map store nine days ago. 212 hand-coloured survey maps (1790s–1840s) were soaked.
- All 212 are in the chest freezers at −20 °C. Keep them there. Do not thaw anything until the method and location are agreed.
- Two ownership groups:
  - **151 maps** are the Archive's own.
  - **61 maps** are the Brekke deposit (2009 loan agreement). Clause 7 says deposited items "shall not be removed from the Archive premises for any purpose without the prior written consent of the Lender". The Brekke executor can only be reached through their solicitor.
- Materials: some maps have watercolour washes in gum arabic. Some have verdigris green along boundaries. Dr Aino Pell (freelance conservator) has examined samples.

## Money

- Conservation budget remaining this year: **€1,100**.
- Valtera Mutual (insurer) reimburses drying up to **€6,000 total**, from any accredited vendor. **[assumed]** Reimbursement is paid after invoice, so the upfront cost falls on us. Confirm this (see step 1).

## Recommended approach (for your decision)

This is my suggestion, not a decision. You own the choice.

1. **Call the Valtera adjuster first.** Confirm:
   - whether they pay the vendor directly or reimburse us after payment;
   - whether the €6,000 cap is per claim;
   - whether Nordlicht and Kestrel are accredited under their scheme;
   - whether in-house drying is covered.
   Nothing else should be committed until this answer is in.
2. **Write to the Brekke executor's solicitor now.** This is the slowest step. Ask for written consent to offsite removal of the 61 deposit maps, naming the vendor and method. Without consent, those 61 maps can only be dried in-house or left frozen.
3. **Get Pell's test done on two of the 151 own maps.** Using own maps avoids the consent question for the test. Ask her also for a view on in-house air-drying for the watercolour and verdigris items.
4. **Split the job if the numbers allow:**
   - *Own 151 maps* via Nordlicht freeze-drying, provided the total including transport is **€5,580 or less** (€5,580 + €420 is exactly €6,000). That means negotiating about €158 off, or having Nordlicht absorb the transport. Confirm the accreditation point from step 1 first.
   - *Brekke 61 maps* wait for solicitor consent before offsite work. If consent is not given soon, consider in-house air-drying for them after Pell approves the method. That is about 3 batches, about 9 days.
5. **Do not reopen Kestrel** unless Pell approves thermal drying for the watercolour items. The 30% discount is worth noting, but it does not solve the technical problem.

## Open questions

- Does Valtera pay vendors directly, and is the €6,000 cap per claim? (Adjuster.)
- Is Nordlicht accredited under the insurer's scheme? (Adjuster and Nordlicht.)
- Will Nordlicht reduce the price to fit the cap for the 151 own maps? (Nordlicht.)
- Will the Brekke solicitor consent to offsite removal, and on what conditions? (Solicitor.)
- Is Pell's test on two own maps feasible on the timeline, and what does it show for verdigris and watercolour? (Pell.)
- Is the in-house air-drying method safe for the watercolour and verdigris items? (Pell.)
- Is the Kestrel 30% discount still valid, and would Kestrel put it in writing? (Only relevant if Pell approves thermal drying.)

## Confidence

- That the Brekke clause rules out offsite work on the 61 deposit maps without consent: **high**. The wording is clear.
- That the recommended split fits the budget and cap: **moderate**. It depends on Nordlicht's price and the insurer's terms.
- That freeze-drying is the right method for the watercolour washes: **low to moderate**. Pell has not confirmed this and her test is not done.
- Overall confidence in the recommended approach: **about 0.6**. Step 1 (the insurer call) could change the picture.
