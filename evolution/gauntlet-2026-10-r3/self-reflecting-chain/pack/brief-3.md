I chair the Corrbridge Fire Authority. On the 21st we vote on closing Millgate and Eastfield fire stations and replacing them with one new station at Harrow Cross, on the ring road. When the plan was first raised, members asked one thing: will the Eastfield estates still get a first engine within 8 minutes on at least 90% of emergency calls? Eastfield station meets that today on 93% of calls. Our head of service delivery, Rhona Baird, wrote the summary below. Can you check it holds up before I circulate it?

---

**Harrow Cross merger: response performance**

Our response model shows the average first-engine time across the borough falling from 7 minutes 40 seconds to 7 minutes 5 seconds with the merger.

The model is reliable. We set its road speeds so that it reproduces the times we expect from a ring-road site like Harrow Cross, and it confirms that Harrow Cross meets the standard.

Harrow Cross is 5.6 km by road from the centre of the Eastfield estates. At our average blue-light speed of 48 km/h that is 5 minutes' driving, plus 90 seconds to turn out: 6 minutes 30 seconds, comfortably inside 8.

Eastfield station has not served the estates well. Members will remember the Corran Mill fire last November, when the first engine took 14 minutes to arrive.

Conclusion: the merger makes the borough's fire cover faster on average, so Eastfield residents will be better protected. I recommend members approve it.
